The World War III: - History foretold?
 

Here is the sequence of the events supposed to occur between the period 1999 and 2012, based on the quatrains of Nostradamus and similar prophecies of other people. Eventually, the events may turn out to be very different. The comments below the quatrains are based both on the current geopolitical situation and some events which are not commensurate with the current situation but may unfold later on. I will try to update as the situation changes.

[I.63] Pestilences extinguished, the world becomes smaller, for a long time the lands will be inhabited peacefully. People will travel safely through the sky, land and seas : then wars will start up again

The scenario described in this quatrain is definitely a modern world scenario as is describes air travel. Air travel became commercially available only after WW-II. World is a small place now with ease of communications. Antibiotics helped man to get rid of many incurable diseases. But, Nostradamus says, wars will start up again.

[IV.32] In those times and places that meat gives way to fish the law of commune will be opposed. The old order will hold strong, then removed from the scene, then all things common among friends put far behind

We have already seen this prophecy coming true. During the early 1990s in Soviet Union and Communist Europe (in those times and places), when the economic condition in these countries deteriorated (meat gave way to fish), Communism (the law of commune) was opposed by the people. The old leaders indeed tried to hold on but ultimately were removed from powers. Ultimately Communism (all things common among friends - equality among friends - comrades) collapsed. This prophecy was understood even before its accomplishment by many interpreters. But the instability caused by collapse of USSR may indirectly be cause of the next great war.

The word "proof" might be related to the proof required for establishing involvement of Osama Bin Laden in the 11th September attack or the nuclear attack and such a proof will be provided by France.

[I.87] Volcanic fire from the center of the earth will cause trembling around the new city: Two great rocks will make war for a long time. Then Arethusa will redden a new river.

[X.49] Garden of the World near the New City, in the road of the hollow mountains. It will be seized and plunged in the tank, forced to drink water poisoned with sulphur

"Garden of the World near the New City" may refer to Central Park.  "in the road of the hollow mountains."  is further reference of what Nostradamus might have envisioned of Central Park's proximity to Manhattan.  "It will be seized and plunged into the tank" refers to an event of "great catastrophe" perhaps terrorists plan to detonate a nuclear bomb in Central Park.  The final line, "forced to drink water poisoned with sulphur "is his metaphor meaning that the water supply around the blast zone, "Central Park" is poisoned as a result. (Thanks to Jim Torino for this interpretation.)

[II.46] After great misery for mankind an even greater approaches when the great cycle of the centuries is renewed. It will rain blood, milk, famine, war and disease. In the sky will be seen a fire, dragging a trail of sparks

After great misery for mankind (World War III) an even greater misery (the so called "End of the World" event - see next quatrain) will occur when "the great cycle of the centuries is renewed". Millennium is the completion of the cycle of the centuries. Thus, this event will take place during the early part of the millennium. At the same time a very bright comet (a fire, dragging a trail of sparks) will be seen, signifying the inauspicious moment. The reference to the comet comes again and again in the prophecies although it is not clear how many comets he speaks of. One comet/asteroid might strike the earth during WW-III while another prominent one will be seen around this time. Nostradamus estimated WW-III to take place anytime between 1999 and 2007. He expected the Arab War (WW-III) to be completed before the 20th century was finished. Probably he is off on all counts by 3 to 4 years. The "Even greater misery" might indicate the event popularly known as the "Three Days of Darkness", which according to Islamic and Christian religious prophecies will be the Judgement Day and bring an end to the present civilization. The sequence of events will be a bit more clear from the Mother Shipton's Prophecies.

[X.74] The year of the great seventh number accomplished, it will appear at the time of the games of slaughter, not far from the age of the great millennium, when the dead will come out of their graves

As for the timing of the "End of the World" event, it will come after the games of slaughter (WW-III). It will be after the "Year of the great Seventh number", which is seven, "not far from the great millennium" i.e. the year 2007. He further says that at this time "The dead will come out of their graves" - as mentioned in the Biblical Prophecy of Apocalypse. Thus the happenings described in this quatrain pertain to the War of Apocalypse or Armageddon prophesized in the Bible. By "it will appear", Nostradamus probably means the appearance or striking of the earth by a comet or meteor which he prophesized in another quatrain and is also mentioned in the Bible, bringing widespread devastation to the world already at war. Thus, the comet might strike the earth when the Third World War is going on.
