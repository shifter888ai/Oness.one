Questions:

Is it possible that another spirit/core occupies my physical body while I am "traveling" through the "astral world"?

It's not possible that a spirit occupies your body while you are away on astral travel. You have the same protection as you have in sleep - while asleep to a certain degree your body is left alone as well, because you have no control about your consciousness; nevertheless you are not endangered.

Why does the room and house I was originally in change?

Because you have no physical sight. Because of the fact that you rolled out of your body, you have the impression to be still close to the physical body. But it is not this way - perhaps you are close to the physical but nevertheless in an other dimension (or how you may call that). Instead still moving in the material surrounding, as you have the vivid impression, you move in an astral reproduction of your room, a kind of astral layer which penetrates the physical counterpart.

Why does the outside area change?

The outside area changes because you are moving in an astral counterpart as told above. But I want to point at an additional and interesting fact:
Your outside area does not change continually but it changes in steps. Everytime you pass a border your surrounding changes in the range of "only a bit" up to "dramatically". Let me just explain what I understand by the term "border". A border is a location which prevents you to have a sight of the coming, like a door, the hook of a wall or a building, a street crossing etc. This changing by steps seems to be the result of a psychic law. Using this law you even can realize a wish for arriving at a special place by means of imaging this location after the border in front of you. Thereby you have a chance to approach your desired location.
