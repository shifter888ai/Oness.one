How To Make Your OBEs More Vivid?

© copyright Alfred Ballabene, Vienna, 2000

To become your OBEs more vivid you need a higher degree of awareness, promoted by increased concentration and higher emotions. To this some valuable hints:

  • If you are out, then increase your body awareness by catching hold of any object,
  • by touching some objects, increasing your touch feeling this way
  • by feeling your feet touching the ground when walking
  • by fixing a spot or any object, which makes your vision more plastic and colorful
  • by going slowly (avoid gliding or flying) so that you look and observe more acurate

When you are then continueing your OBE then repeat from time to time this bodycheck - and don't try to haste because you are eager to collect as much as possible of all these interesting impressions around and in front of you.
