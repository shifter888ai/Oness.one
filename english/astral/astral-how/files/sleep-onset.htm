SLEEP ONSET SUGGESTION

      The sleep onset suggestion method involves hypnogogic (the state between wakefulness and sleep) suggestions.  Basically you will be using self-hypnosis at a time when the mind is most open to the power of suggestion.

      While you are anticipating sleep, instead of letting random inages enter your mind, keep it occupied by visualizing a specific image.  When subtle changes occurr in the image and you are unable to hold it clearly in your mind anymore, sleep is about to take over. 

      At this point, mentally tell yourself that you will have an out of body experience, when you do fall alseep.  Keep saying this mentally until sleep takes over.

      This is the method I used to experience my first ever self-induced OBE!  It worked very well, however I found that it was hard to stay focused to keep my mind awake.  It could have been tiredness taking over...
