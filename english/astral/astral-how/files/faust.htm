Faust's AP Technique

When I want to AP, the first thing I do is stretch out a bit to relax and make my muscles looser. After I do my ritualistic stretching, I then go and use the bathroom to cleanse my system. When I am all stretched and what not, I go around and make sure that my atmosphere in my AP environment is comfortable: dimmed, no sound and a nice temperature. I then lie on my bed and begin to clear my thoughts while doing deep breaths to further relax my body. The breathing process usually lasts anywhere from 5-20 minutes before complete relaxation sets in. When I am in the relaxed state, I feel like my arms and legs are numb, and my breathing goes to a deeper slower breath.

After the relaxed state, I begin to visualize going out of body. I usually think of my body slowly hovering in the air or falling through the bed. Concentration is needed here! Keep this thought in your head and then try and visualize and "feel" your solar plexus, which is a spot below breastbone, swirling. If you get the swirly feeling, then "lead" it to float above you to create your astral-self. This process could take a good amount of time depending on how much concentration you put into it. When I get a good sense that my astral-self is the way I want it, I then try and push myself up to it. This is where you feel the vibes and so on. When the vibes come, my body sometimes jerks a little and my eyes feel like they are going crossed. Here is where I get stuck most of the time because I get disturbed or the vibes just disappear.
