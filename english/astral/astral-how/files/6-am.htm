6:00 AM in the morning method

Set your alarm clock for 6:00 AM and go to bed between 10:00 and 11:00 PM. When you wake up at 6, walk around the house, watch T.V., get on the computer, etc. for 20-30 minutes until you feel fully awake. Then go back to bed, as you drift into sleep try to stay as aware as possible. When you reach the state where you are almost dreaming, yet still awake, then you can let yourself fall to sleep. This may take a few days to work, but you should wake up in the desired trance state about 5-50 minutes after you let yourself fall to sleep. When you find yourself in the desired trance state, where you can’t move and you hear vibrations, you can just roll over out of your body. Be sure to get away from your body because it can pull you back in.
