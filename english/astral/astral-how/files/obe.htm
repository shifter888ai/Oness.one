Inducing O.B.E./Astral Projection

To induce a lucid state of astral projection is to relax while keeping the mind alert and it is necessary that the conscious mind lets go and allows the subconscious mind to roam and have its freedom. There have been many cultures that have sort to find methods to relax the mind to allow astral projection to take place within altered states of heightened senses and awareness while finding quietness and tranquillity of the conscious mind within meditation. There are a number of methods that are practised, to help the conscious mind relax and become more dormant, thus letting go of it's control over the subconscious mind. Some of these methods are done by form of concentration which often leads to a shutdown of the conscious mind and we then enter a more daydream like state.

Method

Firstly to heighten the spiritual abilities, it is always a good idea to start any meditation or induction of astral projection by opening the seven main spiritual chakra energy centers, the method of opening the chakra can be found on the chakra page, or by click on the Chakra link below. When opening the chakra it is also best accompanied by deep breathing and relaxation exercises. Then by focalizing on a small reflective surfaces, such as crystals or silver coins, or alternatively concentrating on and looking into the flame of a candle, is often a good way to induce the conscious mind to shutdown.

This is something that is needed to help astral projection to take place and is not something that is uncommon to most of us, as it is an experience and state of mind that is not dissimilar to a daydream like state, which is exactly the state of mind that we wish to achieve and this state of mind that allows the subconscious mind to drift and have it's freedom. So while focalizing on a reflective surface, allow the eyes to naturally go out of focus, while allowing the mind to be still and empty while of daily thoughts and cares, allowing the more inspirational subconscious mind to drift.

Now you need to fully relax the body so firstly feel your feet, feel them go heavy and relax, then the same with the lower half of your legs from the knees down and then from your hips to your knees feel your body relax and go heavy, then feel the trunk of your body go heavy and relaxed and finally your arms shoulders and then your head all go heavy and relaxed, now take in some deep breaths of uplifting energy and breathe out deeply expelling all your negativity's and allowing yourself to go heavy and relaxed. You should end up feeling in a fairly mesmerized state. If you still find it hard to shut down the conscious minds thoughts then, start in your mind counting backwards from 300 until you drift off and loose count and a stilling of the mind is achieved, one where you should of entered a more relaxed state of mind and slowly be loosing all senses of your surroundings and awareness of the physical body.

Next begin to sense yourself from within your own body, feel your breathing then your heart beating, then feel yourself becoming smaller and smaller within yourself until you slowly go deeper and deeper within your body. Now slowly begin to expand yourself outward, you can have your eyes closed or out of focus, but you should not worry about having physical or mental awareness of your body or surroundings. Now let yourself drift just out in front or above your body, conceive yourself not as being a physical being but a being of light and energy that can move out of the dormant physical body. Allow yourself to expand and float out into the room that surrounds you, have no fear or inhibition, you are safe and no harm can come to you as we are each tethered to the mortal body by an invisible cord called the silver thread and this will draw us back into the mortal body as the experience comes to an end. You may perceive yourself with or without any form while out of body. Now see if you can visualize your surroundings out of body, you may find if you can achieve an O.B.E. that you cannot control your experience or that you do have some control over it. Often you may rise up toward the ceiling and find yourself looking down on yourself.

There are those who can achieve partial out-of-body experiences and can feel part of themselves out of body, but appear to be unable to leave the mind and this often feels as though you are stuck inside your own face wanting to join the rest of your spirit, that can be felt just out or above the body. The reason some get stuck like this is mostly that although they can project the spirit out of body, the conscious mind is still to alert for the spiritual mind to leave and it gets stuck or anchored in, mostly through the conscious mind still being to alert and by the individual trying to hard to get out of body, most of all it does not take conscious concentration to get out of body it takes a willingness to do so with few inhibitions and plenty of belief or self confidence that you can achieve an out of body experience and a relaxing of the conscious minds control of the subconscious and spiritual mind. Therefore if you are finding it hard to leave the physical body then what you need to practice is the relinquishment of the conscious minds grip on the subconscious through meditation techniques, to be able to find the more daydream like state and stilling of the mind and a less consciously aware state.
