Rope Technique

Preparation:

Spend 15-20 minutes getting into your trance state. A deep level of relaxation is best, although not required. Energy work is recommended here. Spend 5-10 minutes drawing energy through your chakras, but don't overdo it.

Sequence:

Imagine a strong rope hanging above or in front of you in whatever position feels most natural. Using your awareness hands, pull yourself up the rope. Feel yourself in your projectable double moving up the rope. Feel yourself leaving your physical body behind you. Your body will want to tense so make sure you stay relaxed--this is all done with the mind. Stay focused only on climbing the rope. Develop a strong, natural rhythm as you climb. Try to feel your projectable double moving up the rope. It's not the movement that makes this technique work; it's the exterior awareness of the climbing action that tricks the mind into accepting and holding a point of awareness outside the body.

Symptoms:
- Dizziness
- Vertigo in the solar plexus area (just below the sternum)
- Localized pressure in the stomach and chest areas
