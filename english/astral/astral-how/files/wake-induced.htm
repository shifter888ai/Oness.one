WAKE INDUCED LUCID DREAMING

1. Use an alarm clock to awaken you in the early morning.
     
2. Then  stay awake for about 5 to 10 minutes, concentrating and keeping the idea in mind of an out-of-body experience.

3. Then concentrate at the beginning of dreaming and by the first signals of the state increase your concentration.  Use an affirmation that you will remain conscious in your dream.  

4. Look for signals in your dreams that tell you that it is not reality.  For example things that don't make sense.

5. Try to change your dream surroundings... dreams of flying have been said to be especially successful in inducing Astral Projections.

6. Actually "feel" your surroundings.  Don't just observe them.  You should feel like you really are flying free as a bird.

7. When you are successful in controlling and really feeling your dreams, you may 'will' yourself to move out-of-body..
