Babel's Interrupted Sleep Technique

Stay up until 1, 2, 3, or 4 a.m. This is no problem for me because I have a slight insomnia problem.

Set the alarm clock so you can sleep for 3 hours and then go to sleep. This will cause you to wake up before the first sleep cycle has ended.

Wake up. You body will be full of sleep hormones. You will be begging to go back to sleep, but you must not. Go get a cup of coffee or a caffeine pill.

Stay awake for 20 minutes. If you do not, you will fall asleep during your attempt. Spend 15 minutes on reading about astral projection. This will get you motivated. Then spend 5 minutes raising energy. Check out Stealth Paladin’s Zykax site for some good energy exercises:
http://www.ineedapage.com/zykax/archive.htm

Assume your meditative position in your bed or armchair. An armchair is better because you are programmed to fall asleep in your bed.

Clear your mind. You can do this by focusing on your third eye. Your brain does not have a lot of issues to process when you have just woken up, so this should not be a problem.

Enter the body-asleep-mind-awake state. I do this by counting from 6 to 10. When I reach 10, my body falls into whatever it felt like the last time I was there. Then, to intensify the state, I think, "For each word I think, my body gets more relaxed." For each of those words, my body does get more relaxed.

Start your get-out-of-body technique. I use the falling technique. As I breathe out, I pretend I am falling rapidly. The ROPE technique should prove efficient too.

The falling sensation will trigger your astral body to pop out. You might feel a falling or compressed sensation in your chest as if you were riding a roller coaster. When I pop out, I float in a sinus wave - downwards, upwards, and then downwards again - landing on my feet in my room. It is an awesome feeling.

Happy projecting. :)
