Developing Your Dream Body

Always begin meditation by relaxing in an environment where you know you will not be disturbed. Also, be sure to shut off the phone.

Put your "protection rituals" into place. You do this by asking the Universe or your guardians for protection during your meditation and intended OBE. Imagine a bubble of protective light around you and your area. You may burn sage, light a candle or enact any other form of ritual that has meaning to you. The point is to tell the Universe and yourself that you are doing this meditation with the specific purpose of revealing your astral self.

Now, try to imagine yourself going through the motions of the day you just had. Project yourself to the morning. Follow yourself through the situations of your day and really ‘see’ yourself. Imagine how you look, what you are wearing etc. Try to get a good image of yourself.

Now use this projected self to replay your day. See yourself at the places you visited. Remember the people with whom you interacted. Put yourself back into those scenes as if you were watching it now from another point of view. Especially visit the high points of your day.

Examine how you feel. Are you satisfied? Did you enjoy your work? Was it a good day? If things are unresolved, try to imagine what you might do to resolve them; play out your day in a different way in your head. Imagine how you would make the day better if you could.

If things were just fine, be at peace in re-playing the images and circumstances of the day which brought you to this night. See yourself there in your meditation. Look at yourself and imagine how you look lying on your bed meditating. See the image of your projected self joining with you in the current moment. Feel you have "caught up" with yourself for today. Be at peace and allow the images to come. Tell yourself you will remember any dreams you have tonight.

Now let everything go and silence your mind. Practice this 'silencing of mind' for this is what will set up the circumstances for an OBE.

If physical sensations such as vibrations or paralysis begin to occur, try to endure them and hold your fear at bay. The best is to ignore them.

You will know if you are reaching the astral by the sounds you are hearing. Roaring ocean, rushing wind, loud machinery, whispering voices, screams etc... Don't be put off by any of these things; remember that you are protected and that you really want to know this.

The important thing in this meditation is to try and hold a clear image of yourself all the way through the whole exercise while you are reflecting on your day. If you can hold a clear image of yourself, it will strengthen your recall.

All we can hope for is to set up the circumstances for an OBE to occur. Keep a pen and notebook or a tape recorder beside your bed to record any images or dreams you can remember at any time during the night or in the morning upon awakening.

Good luck!
