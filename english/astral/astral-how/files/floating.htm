Floating Method

1) this is best attempted at night -when all the vibrations of day and all the interruptions are at a minimum.

2) Do not eat a large meal before attempting this.  No drugs and no alcohol.

3) Lie quietly on your bed or couch -just be comfortable

4) It is okay to have a radio or television going -just don't be distracted by it

5) close your eyes and get your breathing as low and slow as you can.

6) imagine your body relaxing - picture each body part as you mentally move up your body - toes, feet, knees, thighs, torso, chest, shoulders, arms, neck, head.

7) now, if you feel truly relaxed - imagine yourself floating upward- and just feel yourself letting go of the physical and moving slowly upward . start with your feet and go thru your body parts again and imagine them rising out of your physical body.
Don't be scared- many people get frightened at this point -because it's a very unfamiliar feeling-you are in no danger. You may feel a warm "rush" come over you that's another sign it's beginning. Keep concentrating on floating up and out - it will happen. That's it.

8) Don't be disappointed if you don't get out the first time -although many people do,    it can take a little practice.
