Invisible Body Technique

Find a place where you will have the least amount of noise and distractions. The place must also be a place where you feel safe and comfortable. The most obvious choice would be your bedroom on your bed. Try to limit the amount of light coming into the room. I like to have things that glow in the dark while I am attempting an OBE, since glow in the dark pain is very unobtrusive and it provides a good atmosphere. Also, you should not be tired before attempting to OBE, since you will probably fall asleep.

Lie flat and get comfortable.

If you like to listen to music to relax, put the most unobtrusive music you have. If you are going to play music try not to put anything that has jumps in sound. The best music is one that is very mellow and smooth. If you do not have very mellow music, then it is best not to put any music on. I would also suggest that the music is neutral, i.e., it is neither sad nor extremely cheerful. In other words, the music should be conducive to a trance state.

Use a relaxation technique to focus the mind and to center yourself. What you want to achieve is a state where your mind can focus easily on anything and at the same time it is very relaxed and calm. Everyday worries should also not affect the state of your mind nor should they be present. I think the best advice to get rid of everyday worries when they surface, since they always seem to do so, is to pay as much attention that you need to pay to them at the moment so that they no longer worry you. After paying attention to them, they will usually stop bothering you. Everyday worries just want a little attention and then they are satisfied.

I would recommend using a breathing technique combined with a physical technique to get relaxed. The breathing technique will give you the energy necessary to focus the mind and get rid of everyday worries, while the physical technique will get rid of accumulated stresses in the body so that you can more easily detach yourself. It is important to stress the importance of physical exercise or a physical technique, since they make the mind less focused on the body during the trance process. If the body has a lot of accumulated stress, most of the focus will go towards the body. To give you an example, think of the following: have you ever had such a good massage that you feel as if your body has kind of evaporated... well, that is the type of feeling that is very conducive to an out of body experience.

The breathing technique is important because it lets old energy go out of your system and it lets new energy enter the body. This topic is not very well understood by me, but all I know is that it really does work.

Ok, so you are very relaxed after doing the physical technique and the breathing technique and you are now wondering what to do next. Well, the first thing to do is to see how relaxed you are. The state that you want to be in is one of deep relaxation. The state is classified as being somewhere near sleep, but still in waking consciousness. It is very hard sometimes to tell where one really is so don't worry about this if you don't know where you are. The only way really to know what state you are in is to have experiences this state before. One indicator that I have found of being in the "ideal" state is that you feel more energetic and more alive than before. This basically means that you are very relaxed and that your mind is very well focused. It also means that the breathing techniques have given you the kick necessary to jump your consciousness to the next level.

Now try to feel your physical body disappearing. Pay less and less attention to you physical body. Do not try to move your physical body either. Any thought of your body will reanimate it so you must concentrate. Do not worry if you suddenly thought of your body since this will also break your concentration. In other words, smoothly switch focus away from your body and what it is doing. One sign that you have done this successfully is that breathing will come to the forefront of your attention. It will be the only thing remaining from your physical body. You will have to trust your physical body to take care of breathing while you are out of body. Once you have paid so little attention to your body that you feel that it is no longer there imagine that your consciousness is enveloped by an energy bubble. The energy bubble is located between your eyes. Now imagine that energy bubble flying through the middle of your eyes to infinity.

That should have triggered an OBE if all the conditions were right.
