Pinpoint of Light

This technique can be good for people that feel a little scared about projecting. You can simply not look at the pinpoint of light to end the technique so it gives you a lot of control.

Pin-point of light projection technique:

Do all your mental and physical relaxation exercises until you feel in the "zone" where concentration and relaxation are at a maximum for you at the time.

Try to look for a pinpoint of light in front of your visual area while your eyes are closed. If you can not see the pinpoint of light relax more. If you do see the pinpoint of light, then just concentrate on it and try to concentrate on nothing else. The pinpoint of light may appear anywhere on your field of vision. If you notice that it moves very erratically it is because you have not quieted the mind enough so go back to your relaxation exercises.

If you feel like you are going out of body by looking at the pinpoint of light do not put any resistance. The easiest way to do this is to just concentrate on the pinpoint of light.
