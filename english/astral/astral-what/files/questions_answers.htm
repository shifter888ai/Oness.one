Questions & Answers

1. When you use the term "astral projection", do you mean that the astral body is projected to the astral plane?
No. The astral body is already on the astral plane, just as the physical body is on the physical plane. You do not "project" to planes but rather focus or tune in to specific planes. You become aware, or gain a different perspective. When I use the word "projection" I mean the separation of the physical and astral bodies.
 

2. Are there always sensations (such as Monroe's vibrations) before leaving the body? What about sounds?
Some report no sensations or sounds prior to an OBE. They simply slip out of the body. This is usually my case; I slip out easily and instantly with no fanfare. But I have had many experiences where extremely strong vibrations occurred prior to leaving the body, but these vibrations became this powerful usually because of my willing it, vibrations so quick and violent that it was only a matter of time before I would be flung out of body, as if shot from a cannon (see Techniques). These vibrations never hurt. In fact, I sometimes have a mental laughing fit during these atom-shakedowns because the experience itself seems so ridiculous and unnecessary. When I am experiencing these extreme vibrations I hear a very loud roaring or rushing sound, which hits its cresendo just as I am propelled out of body. However, at the beginning of the vibrations I sometimes hear what sounds like crickets.
 

3. What about drugs? Do they help induce OBEs?
I wouldn't know. You may have fleeting visions of other planes or realities but confusion and disorientation are said to be the main by-products of drugs. Don't put your body at risk. If you have a real desire to experience astral projection then that desire (and practice) will get you there.
 

4. After I have my first conscious OBE will I be able to have them from then on?
Usually if you experience it once you will experience it again, if you still have the desire to do so. However, if it was an unpleasant or frightening experience you may never (consciously) project again. If you lose interest in OBEs that, too, may dwindle the number of projections or even stop them all together. Desire and practice are the keys to regular projections.
 

5. Does astral projection prove life after death?
For those who have experienced it the answer is usually an emphatic YES! Finding yourself apart from your physical body and still functioning, still able to see, hear, touch, even smell and taste, still able to do everything and more, how can one deny that life goes on? In most of these people's minds there is no doubt. And in my own as well.
 

6. How can I be sure that I had an out-of-body experience?
When it happens you will know. No one will be able to convince you otherwise. If you were fully conscious during your OBE, you will have no doubts.
 

7. I've heard that some astral projectors have had trouble getting back into their bodies. How common is this?
You should never really have any major problems getting back. When you want to go back -- you're there! However, it is true that at times getting realigned with your physical body can be tricky. The first time it is quite alarming, but don't panic; it's no big deal. If you should find yourself back in your body but unable to move it, the best thing to do is just relax and try to fall asleep, let your consciousness dim. If you leave things alone your astral will align itself with the physical automatically. Before I knew this secret I had a couple of terrible experiences (terrible only because I freaked out). The first time I found my physical body in this paralyzed or incapacitated state I panicked big time, screaming like a baby and tossing my astral body about, ejecting from my body several times and jumping back into it until, finally, I was able to move physically again. I was afraid to go back to sleep!
 

8. Is it easier to go to a place or person out of body?
Usually to a person, if there are emotional ties. However, if there is a place that you feel strongly about that could be just as easy.
 

9. What if another entity takes over my body while I'm gone?
You are the only soul who has the "keys" to your body and only you can activate it unless you willfully give permission to someone else (such as Jane Roberts did with Seth). However, if you have this fear, visualize before a journey that your body is surrounded by white light (God) or make the statement. Besides, you leave your body during sleep every night whether your conscious of it or not, and your body is still in your possession, isn't it?
 

10. If I go out of body when I sleep and I don't recall the experience, where do I go?
Beats me. There are any number of things you could be doing while OBEing, things that are specific and important to you as a person. At times you wander aimlessly in the astral plane, acting out your dreams. Other times you might hook-up with friends, family, and loved ones who have crossed over recently (in your lifetime) or from past lives. You might also be attending Soul School (as my sister calls it), learning from those more advanced than you (Masters) and finding the solutions to your earthly concerns.
 

11. What is the difference between an out-of-body experience and remote viewing?
In an out-of-body experience you are apart from the physical body, receiving visual and other data as you move about to those specific places. With remote viewing you are still in the body, receiving images usually in front of or slightly above the eyes. Remote viewing is like being someplace without being there.
 

12. I know that telepathic communication is done in the astral world, but is normal speaking possible?
The first time I experimented in using my astral vocal cords I couldn't utter a sound; other times I was able to speak but my voice was extremely gravely or guttural. And yet other times I could speak normally or sing better than I could ever in physical life. I don't know the reasons for these variations. I suspect, however, it has to do with my particuliar thought patterns at the time. If my hoarse voice was thought-induced it must have been subconscious for I was certainly surprised by it. I've also tried to make noise by stomping my astral foot to the ground and was surprised to hear a loud reverberating banging. Again, was this a thought-induced sound or a natural law of the astral world? I believe it was the former, just another thought-creation done on an unconscious level. But how strange indeed to hear such a loud banging echo across that otherwise whisper-quiet astral expanse.
 

13. How long can I stay out of my body?
Because there are a lot of unknowns regarding astral projection, I would advise the out-of-body practitioner not to extend the experience by mental force if the physical body is calling you back. If you feel a tug, or know instinctively that it's time to leave, then leave.

14. Do animals survive death? Do they have souls?
I believe so. We had a horse named Tonka that had died from old age. He was a very shy and gentle creature. A week after his death I was astral traveling over our field when I spotted him below, his head down as if he were eating astral grass! When he saw me, he whinnied (I swear I heard this!) as if he were happy to see me. This whole experience could have been a thought-creation; I don't know. I do believe animals survive death, though. It would be a very unfair god indeed that would afford only humans this right. Robert Monroe has also reported something similar: In the out-of-body state he had once seen and recognized three of his pet cats that had died during the previous three years; they were hanging around just outside the house.

"The animals share with us the privilege of having a soul." -- PYTHAGORAS (circa 582-507 B.C.) Greek philosopher, mathematician, mystic, "Father of Vegetarianism"

15. What is it like to see your physical body during an OBE?
It's a freak-show and you're the star of it! Initially it is quite shocking. Because the facial muscles are completely relaxed during sleep, the skin is more loose or drawn downward. I recall the first time I stared at my sleeping face during an OBE -- I looked dead. There is no way to describe the feeling of seeing your body from this vantage point, face to face as it were. You see your body as it really is, for what it is, a machine, a vehicle designed for earthly exploration and experience.
 

16. Does man have other bodies beside the physical and astral?
I don't know for sure yet (I'll get back to you), but man is believed to be equipped with several bodies: the gross physical body, the subtle astral body, and the mental and casual bodies (the causal body residing on the upper mental plane). All your bodies are said to take up the same space, yet vibrate at a different rate. The higher the rate of vibration, the finer or less substantial the body -- the physical body being the most dense, the casual the least; and each designed specifically for (and from) that specific plane.

 

17. How far can one go out of body?
There is no limit to how far one can go away from the physical body. However, there are certain areas that you do not readily have access to unless you are developed or have graduated to those areas. You cannot visit a higher vibratory plane in a lower vibratory body.
 

18. Is it possible to take astral trips with others?
Sure. Astral travel adepts can project from the same or different location and take mini-vacations together to any place of their choosing. They can also assemble group meetings at a specified place, each arriving from a different location.
 

19. Can you touch things out of body?
A complex question. My astral body seems to be more dense on different occasions. This could be due to my thought-patterns, however. Sometimes my astral body feels so dense that passing through walls is impossible. If it becomes too dense and sluggish I will go back to my body, less substantial and I can pass through objects with little or no resistance. I've touched many things during my OBEs: my astral cord, astral tongue, stuck my astral fingers in my astral ears. I've picked up small pieces of paper, leaves, ect. I've grabbed tree branches (usually during desperate crash-landings!), crawled around on my bed, walked up steps, turned doorknobs and opened doors, ect. But the question is, did these objects appear solid to me because of my expectations or beliefs? How solid were these objects really? I've certainly felt the solidity of walls on my astral fingertips, but I've also felt walls give in, too -- just a slight resistance as I passed through -- and yet other times it was as if there were nothing there at all.
 

20. How do I avoid negative areas and entities during an OBE?
First, you should never attempt to leave your body if you are in a bad mood or angry. The Rule of the astral plane is: Like attracts like. If you put out negative vibes, you will attract negative entities. On the other hand, if you hold a loving or uplifting disposition you will likely attract loving entities. If you do encounter lower vibratory creatures, tell them who is boss and tell them to go away. Demand it! If they don't respond accordingly, you can always go back to your body.
 

21. What does the astral plane look like?
One characteristic of the astral plane is its pinpoints of light or starry quality, which is the effect of a higher rate of vibration. The main characteristic is that it is much more vivid than earth. In the best OBEs, it's as if everything sparkles. To gaze upon the astral moon will make you cry. Somehow it seems more real to me. The moon is nearly ever-present when I'm out of body; huge, bright, and always full. To buzz over and in between a stand of trees at night with a sky filled with stars and a majestic moon is almost a sensory-overload. I have thanked God many times while out of body and in body.
 

22. What does the astral body look like?
Like the astral plane, the astral body assumes a luminous starry elegance. The astral body is usually an exact counterpart of the physical body, younger looking, for most, for this is how one usually perceives oneself. But the true form of the astral body does not resemble the physical -- this resemblance is constructed by habit, of present or previous incarnation.
 

23. Why do you prefer the rolling-off-the-bed method of projection over, say, floating out of body?
I use the rolling-off-the-bed method predominantly now because of habit. For the beginning practitioner I think it is best to leave the body in a way that isn't too outlandish -- floating out of body to me was just too freaky a concept at the time. If I had tried it then, most likely any attempts would've been terminated the moment I felt a floating sensation. I'm a baby, what can I say? To illustrate how big a baby I am, I also have this bad habit of putting out a please-don't-bother-me-dead-people-or-you'll-freak-me-out vibe. I know I'm putting out this vibe even though I'm not always conscious of it. This would explain why my trips to the astral plane are nearly always people-free, dead or otherwise.
 

24. What do you think of the "silver cord" concept? Have you seen it?
I have always thought that the idea of a silver cord connecting the physical and astral bodies was a silly one, and I still do, even though I have seen it and touched it. This idea never rang true for me as a natural, God-spawned creation but an idea constructed by man to protect himself, to make him feel better and assured that while astral traveling he is securely tethered to both worlds. After reading about this cord in so many OBE books, I wanted to know if it really existed. So, during an OBE I looked and ... lo and behold, I found it! It was thick and white (not silver) and connected to the back of my astral head. It was as real and tangible as if I were holding a garden hose. However, I still don't buy it. I've had enough experience with thought-creation to know how things can magically appear when one is "programmed" with certain ideas, although I will admit that it is interesting that my cord appeared white rather than silver, this being contrary to all my reading.
