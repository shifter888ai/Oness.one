Theory
 
Brain Waves: Brain wave patterns are categorized by the location of electrical activity in the brain. The brain produces different patterns of electrical activity in the brain depending on whether it is sleep, awake or is active. Within the sleep and awake state, there are many ranges of activity depending on the amount of physical and mental activity being performed.

The out of body state was believed to occur in the EEG pattern Central Delta, which corresponds to deep sleep, but according to research by Charles T. Tart, the state most condusive to an OBE is actually similar to that of Stage 1 and 2 sleep, mixed with some Alpha patterns and REM sleep patterns.

The EEG pattern was only a piece of the puzzle, since entering that state did not guarantee an OBE. There seem to be psychological causes as well as other factors affecting the chances of an out of body experience.

Psychological Causes: Most psychological barriers are basend on the element of fear. The astral realm is something that we do not see everyday and it is not well documented at this time.

The ego also fears that it will loose a part of itself in the trance state and that its idea of the real world will be shatered if there is such a thing as an astral realm.

Most barriers in terms of techniques come in the form of what expectations the person has about the experience. If the person is very attentive about every little change his body goes through and is in the habit of cataloging every little feeling, he will become very distracted in this process and will not enter the trance state and the state before the out of body experience. The mental state that is required before the out of body experience is one of calm concentration.

There are many other psychological causes that may stop one from entering the trance state and triggering the out of body experience, but most of these can be solved by rigorous exercise.
