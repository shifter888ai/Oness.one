Creating Your Own Technique
 
Ingredients:

Combine the following ingredients in a proper atmosphere. See introduction for the creation of an atmosphere.

Add an affirmation to give yourself a goal and to tell the ego that he will still have an existance outside of everyday life. The best affirmations are those that make you feel some type of emotion.

Add some sort of exercise or technique that will get your physical body and mind relaxed. This can get very complex if you wish. If you like complex techniques, this is where you should focus your energy and time.

Add one breathing technique. It can be very simple.

Add one or more of the following ingredients and feel free to mix them: visualization, visualization with motion, deep relaxation, visualization of light in front of eyes, gazing (looking at a point above the body), using energy from the chakras, body asleep mind awake, listening for a sound in your ears and letting the sound grow louder, gazing with motion, consciousness expansion (feeling with your eyes closed beyond your body), etc...
There are many other ingredients to use that I have missed. Feel free to add your own.

There are many ingredients that do not mix properly, but you will have to discover what these combinations are for you.

One ingredient that mixes very well with many others is motion. You can mix it with the light technique that is available on the technique section of this website, you can mix it with gazing, you can mix it with energy work, etc... It goes very well with many other ingredients and it is very useful to keep the mind awake and aware.

Another ingredient that is very useful to some is gazing. Gazing is the process of focusing the attention on a spot somewhere. I will let you to experiment with its uses.

Mix all of the above without any expectations of the outcome. A mind that expects little is better than a mind that is always super charged with expectations and never relaxes. Be open to the experience.

Add a pinch of creativity. If you feel as if something is important for you to do during the technique, then do not be afraid to experiment.

By all means, listen to your self. He knows more about you than you do.
