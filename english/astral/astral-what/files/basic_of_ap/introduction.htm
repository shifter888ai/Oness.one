Introduction
 
This is an introduction to the most important elements that will affect your chances at having an astral projection. The two most important elements are your physical and your mental environments. Your physical and mental environments are never going to be perfect, but every effort should be made so that they are both peaceful.

If you have time to spare, I would recommend that you make yourself an isolated space where you know that you will not be bothered. There are "floatation tanks" in the market that serve as good places where you can try to astrally project. Floatation tanks serve as good places to astrally project since they are quiet and private. Be sure to read more about floatation tanks before deciding on buying one. There are some good links to more information on floatation tanks on the links section of this website.

The elements that one should examine in one's physical environment are the following:

Noise: If you have too much noise around you or if you expect for there to be noise during the time that you are going to try to go out of body, the best idea is to get yourself a pair of ear plugs. Another solution is to listen to music to block outside noise though this is not as recommended as just blocking out all noise by other methods.

Distractions: If you are expecting a call, if you are near other people while trying to astrally project, if you have a cat or a dog nearby, etc... Get rid of all nearby distractions. This means unpluggin the phone, turning off any celular phones, astrally projecting away from pets and people in a private space and just basically getting rid of as many distractions as possible.

Temperature: The temperature of your surroundings should be about 3 degrees colder than 77 degrees F or more if you are going to have a blanket on top of you, and it should be about 1 or 2 degrees colder than 77 degrees F if you are not going to have a blanket. I tend to prefer environments that are a bit cold so I can have a blanket to make me feel warm inside of the cocoon and to also make me forget about my body as it is being warmed inside the cocoon of blankets.

Clothing: Clothing, if worn, should be loose. Tight clothing will not help the body to relax. If you feel comfortable projecting in the nude, then do it.

Light: There should be very little light coming into the room that you are trying to astrally project. Ideally, there should be no light at all. However, it is important to have light in the morning after you have tried to astrally project so one should not make one's room very dark during the whole day. Light is important towards maintaining good health so the room should only be dark at night and not in the day.

The best physical environment is one where one can feel safe and where one will not be bothered any distractions of any kind.

The elements that one wants to watch in one's mental environment are the following:

Fears: This is by far the most difficult mental block to solve. If you have any fears before astrally projecting, you should examine all of them carefully. If you can not answer your own fears, the best solution is to read a book or more about astral projection. Fears are very hard to get rid off.

Affirmations can help to lessen fears and positive thoughts also help. There is no easy way to get rid of fears; it is up to the individual to treat them by themselves or talk to other people about their fears. The worst thing that one can do is to hide one's fears so accept them if you feel their presence and know that fears do serve a purpose, which is to give us energy when we most need it.

Most fears originate from the ego consciousness. The ego is afraid that by going out of body or experiencing something that is out of the ordinary it will loose its identity. Exercises done to reduce the precence of the ego also help in reducing ego induced fears. Telling your ego that it has an existence beyond the body will serve to reasure it and calm it down. This is why Monroe's affirmation begining "I am more than my physical body" is so effective. Here is the full affirmation:

"I am more than my physical body. Because I am more than physical matter, I can perceive that which is greater than the Physical World. Therefore I deeply desire to Expand, to Experience, to Know, to Understand, to Control, to Use such greater energies and energy systems as may be beneficial and constructive to me and to those that follow me. Also I deeply desire the help and cooperation, the assistance, the understanding of those individuals whose wisdom, development and experience are equal to or greater than my own."

Daily Preocupations: Deal with everything that you are worried of or that is concerning you at the time. There is nothing worse than trying to astrally project and remembering that you forgot to feed the dog. Dealing with everything that is worring you will also give you peace of mind when trying to astrally project and your mind will be more focused on the moment.

Exercise, relaxation and a good diet can help to improve your mental environment.
