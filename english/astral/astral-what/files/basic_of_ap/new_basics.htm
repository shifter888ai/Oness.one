New Basics
 
Motion: Motion in terms of bodily feeling and in terms of visualization can be very helpful in achieving an astral projection. Motion helps in visualization by letting the mind be active at the same time that it is in a relaxed state. The mind is a very active being and letting it be active with a predetermined object can take one into the trance state. For example, one can visualize a ball or a cube moving to keep the mind active and to keep the thought current to a minimum. If the mind is properly distracted, the thought current will diminish.

Some people have commented that swimming in the ocean for a long time and letting your body feel the motion of the waves all day long so that as one is going asleep they can still be felt is helpful to relaxing the body and mind due to the energy of the motion experienced.

Energy: Energy is a general term for a type of spiritual, physical and mental energy that is acquired in this plane of existance. A good healthy diet, exercise and an active mind are all that is needed to keep one´s energy high. A good and healthy environment is also needed however to keep the energy high after it is acquired.

There are many practices where energy is acquired through physical exercises. This seems to be the best path to take in terms of energy, since strenous physical exercise strenghtens the body, mind and soul.

Chakras: The practice of activating the chakras might have some effect on letting one astrally project. It is my personal belief that the activation of the chakras is not needed to astrally project. Nevertheless, the practice of activating the chakras and the feeling of this energy is helpful towards letting one become consciouss of one's energy body. For this reason, the activation of the chakras is a helpful practice, but it should be combined with some technique to astrally project. A simple technique will work if one is consciouss of one´s energy body or chakra energy.

Meditative Trance: A meditative trance is just that a trance state. This state can most easily be accessed through a calm mind and a relaxed body; this is why exercise is so important.

Light: Light is just another element in terms of visualization techniques. Its main purpose is to keep the mind distracted while other parts of the mind are working to calm the body and mind down.

Concentration: Concentration consists of quieting the mind so that thoughts seem to come in minimum doses. It is a state where the mind is no longer very actively thinking, but it is in a more observant state.

To achieve this state, one needs to use the will to calm the mind. If one thinks of the mind as a calm pond with every thought as a ripple, then one will get the idea that the mind is very fragile when it comes to disturbing it towards thought and action. To calm the mind all it takes is the will for it to be so and the patience for thoughts to subside. If one tries to calm the mind by using the mind, all that one is doing is creating more ripples in the pond. One needs to let all thoughts pass and to not worry if they will every stop coming. A feeling of detachment towards one thoughts is what is needed to stop their flow.
