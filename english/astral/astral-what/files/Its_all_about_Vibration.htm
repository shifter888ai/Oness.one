It is all about Vibration
 
It is all about Vibration. What divides one level from the another, is vibration, and the vibration of any single being is determined by its focus, its thinking. The less instinctual, the higher the vibration. However, part of the Divine Paradox once more, is that those beings considered to be on level one, closest to matter, the Devic kingdoms and the vast consciousness that generate rivers, mountains and forests, are also of a vibration that allows them to be present in the highest level of the Astral Plane. As your holy book says 'the first shall last and the last shall be first'. What is important to remember, is that this is a model, and as such is a learning tool, a way of communicating how consciousness is organized. However, like all models, there are always exceptions to the rule. It is sufficient to say, that if nine million nine hundred and ninety nine souls do things a particular way, in a Universe of free will, there will almost always be a soul that will create a different experience for themselves. What has been a source of confusion for many of you are the differing reports that you have received concerning descriptions of the 'afterlife' or of 'heaven'. In a Universe that is springs forth out of thought, it is entirely possible that all reports, although possibly contradictory, are correct. Although we have described different levels, which are differentiated by consciousness and vibration, within these levels are vast areas of belief system. Souls of similar belief gather together and create worlds within the Astral plane that reflect the their last identity on the Earth plane. In other words, a Catholic heaven exists, a Hindu heaven exists. Each person on death, when being met by a guide to assist them, can have encounters with Jesus, Shiva, Abraham, The Buddha and so forth. These encounters take place when strong belief in such encounters summons forth the experience. Heaven can be a place of luxuriously green meadows with flowers, or a place of serious study, it all depends on your expectation. When you read reports of near death experience or the stories told you by mediums and those who are able to communicate with the 'dead', do not take what is said as undeniable fact, but recognise that one unique experience is being communicated to you.
