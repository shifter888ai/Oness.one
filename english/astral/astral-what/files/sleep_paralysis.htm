Sleep Paralysis - Natural Gateway?

Are sleep paralysis and OBEs related?  Can sleep paralysis be used as a mechanism to help induce Astral Projection?  First we must take a look at what sleep paralysis actually is, before I can fully answer those questions.

      Each night when we go to sleep and enter the REM state (this is when we dream), a hormone is secreted that supresses our ability to act out our dreams.  This is the bodie's own defense against dangerous actions that would be beyond our waking control.

      Normally, the chemicals wear off in our system before we awaken, so we are never aware of the side effects.  Sometimes however, a person can wake up before this happens, and they then become aware of the paralysis caused by the hormone.  Usually that person is still experiencing the state between sleep and full wakefullness, called hypnopompia.  Since any feeling of paralysis can be inherantly frightening, the brain which is still functioning between the states of sleep and awake tends to add to the feeling of terror with hypnopompic hallucinations, usually dark figures which are associated with evil. 

     I have experienced sleep paralysis many times, and it was very frightening as a child.  I also saw the dark figures, but after I learned that they were my mind playing tricks, and what I was feeling was normal, I became more comfortable with experiencing it, and the dark figures went away.  I still however awoke to sleep paralysis on occasion.  I would close my eyes, and try to relax completely instead of fighting it, and learned that it was more like a gateway between the physical and non-physical. 

      When we are in sleep paralysis, I believe that we are laying on the verge between two worlds.  Since it is believed that when we dream we are really astrally projecting, this only further strengthens the point.  When in this state, if you can overcome the overwhelming fear, you are really already halfway to a full conscious OBE, maybe further.  It requires very minimal effort to achieve full seperation from this point, and to stay fully aware and conscious.

      To date, this is how I achieve the majority of my successful conscious obes.  Getting past the fear took alot of courage, but understand what was happening to my body physically helped.  I hope by explaining it here will help you, and if you ever experience sleep paralysis again, that you can use it to your advantage instead of it being a frightening experience.

      Still, if you find yourself in s.p. and are too frightened to attempt astral projection, the easiest way out of the paralysis is to focus on your pinky finger and try to move just your finger, then your hand, then your wrist, and by then usually you've come out of it.
