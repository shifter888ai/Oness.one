The 7 Planes

Occultists believe that there are seven different planes of existence, and some of these can be accessed during astral travel. Below their names and a short description of each is given.

1. Physical Plane - This is the everyday plane. Included in the physical plane is the "etheric plane", which would explain why people experiencing OBEs stay close to the earth plane, since in an OBE they are holding a lot of etheric matter. This plane can be visited during astral projection.

2. Astral Plane - This is where all of our hopes, desires, and dreams become reality, thus making it the emotional plane. You would find many different creatures here, such as fairies and devas. Things here are also changeable. Time and space are distorted in this plane, so traveling into the future or the past is pretty easy. An hour in the astral can be five minutes on the earth plane. Simply imagining something while you are in the astral plane can  cause it to form.

3. Mental Plane - This is a plane full of color and rainbows. If you find yourself in this plane, don't try to rationalize it all because it is pretty much an impossible task. This plane, as can be taken from its name, is full of thoughts and ideas, manifested as real objects. You may be traveling through your own thoughts or you could be traveling through another's thoughts. This plane overlaps a lot with the astral plane. Time here is distorted even more than it is in the astral plane. The mental plane is harder to reach than the previous two, but nonetheless it is achievable.

4. Buddhic Plane - This is a very powerful plane full of love and peace. If you ever manage to reach this plane, you will probably never want to come back from it. In the Buddhic plane, you would feel as if you are one with everything, so individuality would have no meaning to you anymore. Occultists describe this as being the last plane that we as humans can reach.

5. Atmic Plane - Here there is no individuality. This is a very spiritual plane, and is filled with radiant silver light that would be impossible for us as humans to look at without being blinded, but in reaching this plane, it would seem very bright and yet soft and filled with love. Some people have claimed to have reached this plane, but I would not know for sure if it really is possible, for I have never been there or gotten close to being there.

6. Anupadaka Plane - This is definitely a plane that would drive a person mad. Everything here is part of everything else, like great paradoxes all tied together. Millions of beings are said to reside here.

7. Adi Plane - The highest plane of the seven. We could never understand this plane with our limited thinking. This is the realm of Everything and complete Oneness.
　

There are also believed to be subplanes in between each of the seven main planes. Some say there are seven of these for each plane.
