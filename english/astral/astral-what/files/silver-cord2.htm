The Silver Cord

Many people have heard of or have seen the infamous silver cord during an OBE.  Still, others have not.  Be assured though that whether or not you see it, it is and always will be there, until the day you die.  It is the connection or 'life force' between the physical and nonphysical worlds. 

    Imagine millions of tiny strands wound together, infinately stretchable, carrying the precious cargo of your energy scource, nurturing you while in this physical existence.  Some describe it as  being silver and shimmering, and maybe even semi-opaque.  It follows you wherever you go while out of body or in, attached to you by the back base of your neck.

    The cord can not be severed while you are out of body, likewise while in your physical body, with exception to your physical death.  You can also travel to anywhere out of body without exceeding it's limits.  Remember that it is infinate by our physical perception of space.

AN EXERCISE TO  ACTUALLY FEEL THE PRESENCE OF THE ENERGY CARRIED BY THE SILVER CORD:

    Take your hand (left or right, it doesn't matter), and hold it palm towards the back base of your neck, about 4 inches away.   After a few moments you will feel a tingling and maybe warmth in your hand.  (and the warmth can not be attributed to blood flow, as your heand is above your heart)  It can be either strong or very weak depending on your perceptiveness, but it will be there.  You are actually feeling the energy carried to you by your silver cord.
