The Planes of Nature & the Chakras

I am currently trying to reconcile my own understanding of the planes of nature as explained by the great theosophist C.W. Leadbeater with the psychological functions of the 11 major chakras as taught for Pranic Healing.

The Planes of Nature

In Leadbeater’s book “Man Visible & Invisible” he uses the following diagram to represent the planes of nature.  Note I have cropped out plane 6 (the plane beyond nirvana) and plane 7 (the greater plane beyond nirvana) as I am not able to relate them to the chakras, or anything else for that matter!

Rupa means having form & arupa meaning formless, so we have the lower mental plane being of concrete thought, and the higher being of abstract thought.

The Chakras

The functions of the major chakras that I feel are relevant are:

Basic – self-survival, self-preservation
Sex – procreation
Navel – instinct of knowing
Solar Plexus - lower emotions (courage, anger, aggression)
Heart – higher emotions (mercy, compassion, loving kindness)
Throat – concrete mind
Ajna – abstract mind
Forehead – lower intuition
Crown – higher intuition

How Does This Help Us Understand Our Evolution

Hmmmmm, this is where I’m feeling pretty muddy & would love some feedback!

Our conciousness  resides on several planes simultaneously, for the majority of us I would say the physical, astral & mental planes.  As to where humanities consciousness is centred right now, I believe it to be somewhere around the navel and solar plexus.  My reasoning for this is that the navel chakra’s “instinct of knowing” is group consciousness, the shared consciousness of the society in which you live.  Certain behaviour and rules are accepted as given, we live by them largely without question.  Our society is also ruled by predomently men, and characteristics of strength, power, courage, and unfortunately aggression and intimidation reign supreme.  Fear is an enormous incentive in our society, fear of not having enough material wealth, fear of not being accepted by our peers etc. etc.

Of course, we also possess feelings of basic self-survival & the sex chakra’s need to procreate, but these feelings do not control us in modern society as food & shelter needed for survival is something that is easy enough to come by that it does not dominate our lives.  A decision made not to have children is quite common and does not limit one’s ability to function in society, in fact for a women may vastly increase her ability to achieve positions of power & influence as do those with a highly activated solar plexus chakra. 

We are also guided by feelings of love and compassion, especially in our family relationships & friendships.  Unfortunately the domination of the heart often rapidly drops back to the solar plexus, feelings of possession of others, anger or fear of rejection, enormous, debilitating fears relating to the thought of our loved ones suffering.  How wonderful it will be when we drop down to the solar plexus only when required for basic survival, and live through our hearts with much greater access to the consciousness of the higher chakras.

The mental planes greatly assist with science, technology, invention, the arts, philosophy etc., and while certainly play a major role in society, are the domain of the few.  Those in position of power largely control the outcomes of the mental energies spent, as being the providers of funding (be it government or private), the makers and enforcers of law, and would manipulate these outcomes to serve their own needs.

Lastly the buddhic plane, home of intuition.  This has very little influence in our society.  Those who operate at this level are regarded as being flakey, lacking in intelligence & certainly are not rewarded much respect.  The large majority of religious leaders do not operate from these centres, more often through the solar plexus and the heart
