Lessons in Theosophy


Lesson 24, What next?



      After hearing about Theosophical philosophy, and after wanting to live a life worthy of the Path, the question is sometimes asked: What next?


-- Read Books --


      For many people, Theosophy consists of reading books in their own spare time. Thousands of books have been written about Theosophy. Lesson 23 has a list of web sites with entire viewable books, periodicals, bookstores, libraries, etc.



-- Join a Study-Group --


      Most Theosophical activity is in study meetings and lectures. To find a group in your area, consult the list of Theosophical organizations in lesson 23.


-- Start a Study-Group --


      Many areas do not have Theosophical study-groups. If you have interestd people in your area, you may want to start your own study-group. Information and materials for starting a study-group are available.

-- Final Words --


      It is hoped that Theosophy can give answers to questions that have gone un-answered to the reader. It is hoped that Theosophy can cast light upon the inherent similarities of all the major religons, especially in light of the idea that all major religions come from the same Source. To end, this web page, and Theosophy itself, are dedicated.


~~~


      “A clean life, an open mind, a pure heart, an eager intellect, an unveiled spiritual perception, a brotherliness for one’s co-disciple, a readiness to give and receive advice and instruction; a loyal sense of duty to the Teacher, a willing obedience to the behests of Truth, once we have placed our confidence in and believe that Teacher to be in possession of it; a courageous endurance of personal injustice, a brave declaration of principles, a valiant defence of those who are unjustly attacked, and a constant eye to the ideal of human progression and perfection which the Secret Science depicts — these are the golden stairs up the steps of which the learner may climb to the Temple of Divine Wisdom.”

- H.P.Blavatsky
