Yoga Sutras of Patanjali

Translation by Rama Prasada


The First Chapter: On Samadhi.

  1. Now a revised text of Yoga.
  2. Yoga is the restraint of mental modifications.
  3. Then the seer stands in his own nature.
  4. Identification with modification elsewhere.
  5. The modifications are five-fold, painful and not-painful.
  6. Real Cognition, Unreal Cognition, Imagination, Deep Sleep and Memory.
  7. Perception, Verbal Cognition and Inference are real Cognitions.
  8. Unreal Cognition is the knowing of the unreal, possessing a form not its own.
  9. Imagination is followed in sequence by verbal expression and knowledge, and is devoid of objective substratum.
  10. Sleep is the mental modification which has for its objective substratum, the cause of non-existence.
  11. Memory is the not stealing away along with objective mental impressions (retained) (i.e., the reproducing of not more than what has been impressed upon the mind).
  12. They are restrained by practice and desirelessness.
  13. Of these, practice is the effort to secure steadiness.
  14. And this is firmly rooted, being well-attended to for a long time without interruption and with devotion.
  15. Desirelessness is the consciousness of supremacy in him who is free from thirst for perceptible and scriptual enjoyments.
  16. The same is Higher, when there is indifference to the "qualities" due to the knowledge of the Purusha.
  17. The Cognitive Trance is accompained by the appearances of philosophical curiosity, meditation, elation and egoism.
  18. Preceded by the constant repetition of the notion of cessation is the other; in which the residual potencies only remain.
  19. Is caused by Objective Existence for the Videhas and Prakritilayas.
  20. For others it is preceded by faith, energy, memory, trance and discernment.
  21. Proximate for those whose consciousness of supremacy is keen.
  22. A further also differentiation by mild, middling and intense.
  23. Or, by feeling the omnipresence of God (Ishvara).
  24. Ishvara is a distinct Purusha, untouched by the vehicles of affliction, action and fruition.
  25. In Him the seed of the omnipresence is not exceeded.
  26. He is the Teacher of the Ancients too, not being limited by time.
  27. The sacred word connotes Him.
  28. Its repetition and the understanding of its meaning.
  29. Thence the understanding of the individual self and the absence of obstacles too.
  30. Disaese, langour, indecision, carelessness, sloth, sensuality, mistaken notion, missing the point, instability, - these causing distraction are the obstacles.
  31. Pain, despair, shakiness, inspiration and expiration are the companions of these distractions.
  32. For their prevention, habituation to one Truth.
  33. By cultivating habits of friendliness, compassion, complacency and indifference towards happiness, misery, virtue and vice (respectively) the mind becomes pure.
  34. Optionally, by the expulsion and retention of breath.
  35. Or, Higher sense-activity appearing, causes mental steadiness.
  36. Or, the state of painless lucidity.
  37. Or, the mind having the desireless, for its object.
  38. Or, having the knowledge of dream and sleep as its object of study.
  39. Or, by meditation according to one's predilection.
  40. His power reaches down to the minutest and up to the largest.
  41. Becoming like a transparent crystal on the modifications disappearing, (the mind acquires) the power of thought-transformations (samapatti), the power of appearing in the shape of whatever object is presented to it, be it the knower, the knowable or the act of knowing.
  42. There, the thought-transformation in which the options of word, meaning and idea are mixed up, is called Indistinct (verbal).
  43. Distinctive (wordless) thought-transformation is that in which the mind shines out as the object alone on the cessation of memory, and is as it were devoid of its own nature.
  44. By this the meditative and the ultra-meditative, having the subtle for their objects, are also described.
  45. And the province of the subtle reaches up to the noumenal.
  46. They are the seeded trance only.
  47. The undisturbed flow of the ultra-meditative causes Subjective Luminosity.
  48. Therein the faculty of Essential Cognition.
  49. It has different objects from those of verbal and inferential cognition, as it refers to particulars.
  50. Residual potencies born therefrom impede other residual potencies.
  51. All being suppressed, by the suppression of that too comes the seedless trance.

The Second Chapter: On the Practice of Yoga.

  1. Purificatory action, study and making God the motive of action, IS the Yoga of action.-52
  2. For the purpose of bringing about trance and for the purpose of attenuating afflictions.-53
  3. The afflictions are Nescience, Egoism, Attachment, Aversion and Love of Life.-54
  4. Nescience is the field for the others, whether dormant, alternated or fully operative.-55
  5. Nescience is the taking of the non-eternal, the impure, the painful and the not-self to be the eternal, the pure, the pleasurable and the self.-56
  6. Egoism is the appearance of identity in the natures of the subjective power of consciousness and the instrumental power of seeing.-57
  7. Attachment is the sequential attraction to pleasure.-58
  8. Aversion is the sequential repulsion from pain.-59
  9. Flowing on by its own potency, established all the same even in the wise, is Love of Life.-60
  10. These when put potential, are destroyed along with the passing out of activity.-61
  11. Their modifications are destroyed by meditation.-62
  12. The vehicle of actions has its orign in afflictions, and is experienced in visible and invisible births.-63
  13. It ripens into life-state, life-experience and life-time, if the root exists.-64
  14. They have pleasure or pain as the fruit, by reason of virtue or vice.-65
  15. By reason of the pains of change, anxiety and habituation and by reason of the contrariety of the functioning of the 'qualities', all indeed is pain to the discriminating.-66
  16. Pain not yet come is the avoidable.-61
  17. The Conjunction of the knower and the knowable is the cause of the avoidable pain.-68
  18. The knowable is of the nature of illumination, activity and inertia; it consists of the elements and the powers of sensation, action and thought; its objects are emancipation and experience.-69
  19. The specialised, the Unspecialised the Undifferentiated phenomenal and the noumenal are the stages of "the qualities".-70
  20. The seer is consciousness only; even though pure, he cognizes ideas by imitation.-71
  21. For his purpose only is the being of the 'knowable'.-72
  22. Although destroyed in relation to him whose objects have been achieved, it is not destroyed, being common to others.-73
  23. Conjunction is that which brings about the re-cognition of the natures of the power of owing and the capacity of being owned.-74
  24. Nescience is its effective cause.-75
  25. Removal is the disappearance of conjunction on account of its disappearance that is the absolute freedom of the knower.-76
  26. The means of the removal is discriminative knowledge undisturbed.-77
  27. His discrimination becoming final at each stage, sevenfold.-78
  28. On the destruction of impurity by the sustained practice of the accessories of Yoga, the right of wisdom reaches up to discriminative knowledge.-79
  29. Restraint, Observance, Posture, Regulation of breath (Pranayama), Abstraction, Concentration, Meditation and Trance are the eight accessories of Yoga.-80
  30. Of these the restraints are: Abstinence from injury (Ahimsa); Veracity; Abstinence from theft; Continence; Abstinence from avariciousness.-81
  31. They are the Great Vow, universal, and not limited by life-state, space, time, and circumstance (Samaya).-82
  32. The observances are Cleanliness, Contentment, Purificatory action, study and the making of the Lord the motive of all action.-83
  33. Upon thoughts of sin troubling, habituation to the contrary.-84
  34. The sins are the causing of injury to others and the rest. They are done, caused to be done and permitted to be done; they are preceded by desire, anger and ignorance; they are slight, middling and intense; their result is an infinity of pain and unwisdom; thus comes the habit of thinking to the contrary.-85
  35. (The habit of) not causing injury being confirmed, hostilities are given up in his presence.-86
  36. Veracity being confirmed, action and fruition become dependents.-87
  37. (The habit of) not-stealing being confirmed, all jewels approach him.-88
  38. Continence being confirmed, vigour is obtained.-89
  39. Non-covetousness being confirmed, the knowledge of the how of births.-90
  40. By cleanliness, disinclination to one's body, and cessation-of contact with others.-91
  41. And upon the essence becoming pure, come high-mindedness, one-pointedness, control of the senses and fitness for the knowledge of the self.-92
  42. By contentment the acquistion of extreme happiness.-93
  43. By purificatory actions, the removal of impurity and the attainments of the physical body and the senses.-94
  44. By study comes communion with the desired deity.-95
  45. The attainment of trance by making Ishvara the motive of all actions.-96
  46. Posture is steadily easy.-97
  47. By slackening the effort and by thought-transformation as infinite.-98
  48. Thence cessation of disturbance from-the-pairs of opposites.-99
  49. Regulation of breath (Pranayama) is the stoppage of the inspiratory and expiratory movements (of breath) which follows, when that has been secured.-100
  50. Manifestation as external, internal and total restraint is regulated by place, time and number; and thus it becomes long in duration and subtle.-101
  51. The fourth is that which follows when the spheres of the external and internal have been passed.-102
  52. Thence the cover of light is destroyed.-103
  53. And the fitness of the mind for concentration.-104
  54. Abstraction (Pratyahara) is that by which the senses do not come into contact with their objects and follow as it were the nature of the mind.-105
  55. Thence the senses are under the highest control.-106

The Third Chapter: On Attainments (Vibhutis or Siddhis).

  1. Concentration is the steadfastness of the mind.-107
  2. The continuation of the mental effort (to understand) is meditation (dhyana).-108
  3. The same when shining with the light of the object alone, and devoid, as it were, of itself, is trance (or contemplation, Samadhi).-109
  4. The three together are Samyama.-110
  5. By achievement thereof comes the visibility of the Cognition.-111
  6. Its application is to the planes.-112
  7. The three are more intimate than the preceding.-113
  8. Even that is non intimate to the seedless.-114
  9. The suppressive modification is the conjunction of the mind with the moment of suppression (nirodha), when the outgoing and suppressive potencies disappear and appear respectively.-115
  10. By potency comes its undisturbed flow.-116
  11. The trance modification of the mind is the destruction and rise of all-pointedness and one-pointedness respectively.-117
  12. Thence again comes the mental modification of one-pointedness, when the subsiding and rising cognitive acts are similar.-118
  13. By this are described the changes of characteristic (dharma), secondary quality (lakshana), and condition (avastha) in the objective and instrumental phenomena.-119
  14. "The object characterized" is that which is common to the latent, the rising and unpredictable characteristics.-120
  15. The distinctness of succession is the reason for the distinctness of modifications.-121
  16. By Samyama over the threefold change, comes the knowledge of the past and the future.-122
  17. The word, the object and the idea appears as one, because each coincides with the other; by Samyama over their distinctions comes the knowledge of the sounds of all living beings.-123
  18. By bringing residual potencies into consciousness, the knowledge of previous life states (Jati).-124
  19. Of the notions, the knowledge of other minds.-125
  20. By Samyama overthe form of the body, on perceptability being checked, and thus there being no contact with the light of the eye, comes disappearance.-126
  21. Karma is either fast in fruition or slow; by Samyama over these comes knowledge of death; or, by portents.-127
  22. Over friendliness, &c., the powers.-128
  23. Over the powers, the powers of elephant, &c.-129
  24. The knowledge of the subtle, the veiled, the remote, by directing the light of higher sense-activity towards them.-130
  25. By Samyama on the sub, knowledge of the regions.-131
  26. On the moon, the knowledge of the starry systems.-132
  27. On the pole-star the knowledge of their movements.-133
  28. On the plexus of thenevel, the knowledge of the system of the body.-134
  29. In the pit of the throat, subdual of hunger and thirst.-135
  30. On the tortoise tube (kurmanadi), steadiness.-136
  31. In the coronal light, vision of the Perfected Ones.-137
  32. Or, all knowledge by prescience (prathibha).-138
  33. In the heart, the knowledge of the mind.-139
  34. Experience consists in the absence of the notion of distinction between the Purusha and Objective Essence, which are really quite distinct from each other, because it exists for another. By Samyama on his own object, comes the knowledge of the Purusha.-140
  35. Thence proceed prescience, higher hearing, touch, vision, taste and smell.-141
  36. They are obstacles to trance, but perfections to theout going mind.-142
  37. The mind may enter another body, on relaxation of the cause of bondage, and by knowledge of the passages of the mind.-143
  38. By mastery over UDANA, ascension and non-contact with water, mud, thorns &c.-144
  39. By mastery over Samana comes effulgence.-145
  40. By Samyama over the relation between Akasha and the power of hearing, comes the higher power of hearing.-146
  41. By Samyama on the relation between the body and the Akasha, or by attaining to (the state of thought transforming as) the lightness of cotton, &c., passage through space (Akasha).-147
  42. Actual passing out and acting outside the body is the Great Excorporeal; by that is destroyed the veil of light.-148
  43. By Samyama on the gross (sthula), the substantive (svarupa), the astral (sukshma), conjunction (anvaya) and purposefulness (arthavattha), is obtained mastery over the elements (bhutas).-149
  44. Thence the manifestation of attenuation (anima) and the other (powers); as also the perfection of the body and non-resistance by their characteristics.-150
  45. The perfection of the body consista in beauty, grace, strength and adamantive hardness.-151
  46. By Samyama over the act, the substantive appearance, the egoism, the conjunction and the purposefulness (of sensation) comes mastery over them.-152
  47. Thence comes quickness of the mind, un-instrumental perception and mastery over the Pradhana (First Cause).-153
  48. To him who recognizes the distinction between consciousness and pure objective existence comes supremacy over all states of being and omniscience.-154
  49. The seed of bondage having been destroyed by desirelessness even for that, comes absolute independence (Kaivalya).-155
  50. When the presiding deities invite, there should be no attachment and no smile of satisfaction; contact with the undesirable being again possible.-156
  51. By Samyama over the moments and their sucession, comes knowledge born of discrimination.-157
  52. Two similars are thereby distinguished when not separately distinguishable by genus, differentia and position in space. -158
  53. And it is the intuitional; hat everything for its sphere of operation; has no succession. This is the entire discriminative knowledge.-159
  54. When the purity of the Objective Essence and that of the Purusha become equal, it is absolute independence.-160

The Fourth Chapter: On Absolute Independence (Kaivalya).

  1. The attainments are by birth, drugs, incantations, purificatory action (tapas) or trance.-161
  2. Change to another life-state by the filling up of the creative cause (Prakrityapura).-162
  3. The creative causes are not moved into action by any incidental cause; but that pierces the obstacle from is like the husbandman.-163
  4. Created minds proceed from egoism alone.-164
  5. There being difference of activity, one mind the director of the many.-165
  6. Of these, the meditation-born is free from the vehicles.-166
  7. A Yogi's karma is neither white nor black; of the others is is three-fold.-167
  8. Thence proceed the residual potencies competent to bring about their fruition alone.-168
  9. Memory and potential residua being the same in appearance, there is sequential non-interruption, even when there is distinction of life-state, locality and time.-169
  10. And there is no-beginning for them, thedesire to live being eternal.-170
  11. Being held together by Cause, Motive, Substratum and Object, they disappear on the disappearance of these.-171
  12. The past and the future exist in reality, there being difference of the paths of being of the characteristics.-172
  13. They are manifested and subtle, and of the nature of the qualities.-173
  14. The reality of the object on account of the unity of modification.-174
  15. There being difference of mentality in the case of the external object the same, their ways of being are different.-175
  16. And if ab object dependent upon one mind were not cognized by that, would it then exist ? -176
  17. The mind needing to be coloured thereby an object may be known or unknown.-177
  18. To its lord, the Purusha, the modifications of the mind are always known on account of unchangeability.-178
  19. It is not self-illuminating, being the knowable.-179
  20. Nor can both be cognized at the same time.-180
  21. In the case of being knowable by another mind, there will be too many wills-to-know the Wills-to-know; and there will be confusion of memories.-181
  22. Consciousness knows its own Will-to-be by transforming its appearance, through not-itself moving from place to place.-182
  23. The mind being coloured by the knower and the knowable is omni-objective (sarvartha).-183
  24. And the mind exists for another, also because it is variegated by innumerable residua, inasmuch as it acts by combination.-184
  25. For the seer of the distinction, ceases the curiosity as to the nature and relation of the Self.-185
  26. Then the mind inclines towards discrimination and gravitates towards absolute independence (kaivalya).-186
  27. In the brakes arise other thoughts from residua.-187
  28. Their removal has been described like that of the afflictions.-188
  29. Having no interest left even in the Highest Intellection, there comes from constant discrimination the trance known as the Cloud of Virtue.-189
  30. Thence the removal of actions and afflictions.-190
  31. The knowable is but little then, because of knowledge having become infinite, on account of the removal of all obscuring impurities.-191
  32. By that, the qualities having fulfilled their object, the succession of their changes ends.-192
  33. Succession is the uninterrupted sequence of moments, cognized as distinct on the cessation of evolutionary change.-193
  34. Absolute freedom comes when the qualities becoming deviod of the object of the Purusha, become latent; or the power of consciousness becomes established in its own nature.-194
