Jane Roberts - the author of the book “The Seth Material” - was a person who in a “trance” state spoke as a personality called Seth. Seth calls Jane's personality Ruburt. Jane sometimes goes into a different trance state where she speaks as “Future Seth”.

 

简·罗伯兹是《赛斯资料》一书的作者，她在“出神”状态下以一个叫作赛斯的人格说话。赛斯将简的人格称作拉伯特。有时，简进入一种不同的出神状态，她又作为“未来赛斯”说话。

 

All of the answers and information herein are the words of Seth (or Future Seth) which I have reordered and revised to give continuity to my understanding of the material. I think the main thing lacking in my condensation is Seth's sense of humor. I highly recommend Seth/Jane's books!

 

为了使对材料的理解具有连续性，这里所有的回答和信息是经过我重新整理、修订的赛斯（或未来赛斯）语录。我想，我的缩写失去的主要是赛斯的幽默感。我竭力向你推荐赛斯/简的书籍！

 

Seth Speaking About His Purpose

赛斯谈他的意图

 

Painting by Robert Butts

 

I come here, as an (humorously) “endearing” personality, with characteristics to which I hope you can relate. These characteristics are mine, and I am who I say I am, and yet the Seth you know is but a small part of my reality, the one that has been physical, and can relate to your problems.

 

我到这里来，作为一个（幽默地）“可爱的”人格，具有我希望你们能理解的个性。这些个性是我的，而我是我所说的人，但你们所知道的赛斯只是我的现实的一小部分，他曾经为人，并能理解你们的问题。

 

There exists what could almost be compared to a psychological and psychic warp in dimensions where Ruburt's personality is an apex point at which communication can take place.

 

存在着一个差不多可以比作心理的和精神的维度弯曲，拉伯特的人格是这里的一个端点，这里可以进行交流。

 

We want to deal with the nature of reality as it exists within your camouflage system and within other systems, and to study the overall characteristics that pertain to it. We will discuss the interrelationship that exists between all systems of reality, including certain contact points that include them all. These various points can be mathematically deduced, and will, in some future of yours, serve as contact points, taking the place of space travel in some cases.

 

我们想讨论现实的本质，它存在于你们的伪装系统之内，也存在于其他系统之内，并且要研究它的全部特性。我们将讨论现实中所有系统之间的相互关系，包括包含所有系统的某些接触点。可以对这些不同的点进行数学演绎，并在你们将来的某个时候，它们将被用作联络点，以在某些情形中取代太空旅行。

 

We will be discussing the laws of the inner universe. They are attempts to explain in words the nature of inner reality. These single-dimensional statements are more than are given to most, and, under the circumstances that we must work, are the best approximation that can be made of the basic fact beneath any existence.

 

我们将讨论内在宇宙的法则。这是用语言解释内在现实的本质的尝试。这些单维的陈述远非多数人所了解，并且，在我们必须身处其中的环境中，这是可以做到的对在任何存在下的基本事实的最近似的陈述。

 

As words would give little hint of the reality of color or sound to someone who did not experience these, so words can only give insight into the nature of reality.

 

对于瞎子和聋子来说，语言无法暗示色彩和声音的现实，所以，语言只能给予对现实的本质的洞察。

 

I have been sent to help you, and others have been sent through the centuries of your time, for as you develop you form new dimensions, and you will help others.

 

我被派来帮助你们，在你们时代的数个世纪中，已有其他人被派遣来帮助你们，因为当你们发展了，你们就形成新的维度，你们也会去帮助其他人。

 

Using your free will, you have made physical reality into something quite different than what was intended. You have allowed the ego to become overly developed and specialized. You were here to work out problems and challenges, but you were always to be aware of your own inner reality, and of your nonphysical existence. To a large extent you have lost contact with this. You have focused so strongly upon physical reality that it become the only reality that you know.

 

运用你们的自由意志，你们已造就了与原先意图大不相同的物质现实。你们让自我过度发展、过度专门化。你们在这里是要解决问题和挑战，但是，你们应始终意识到你们自己的内在现实，以及你们的非肉身存在。大而言之，你们已经失去和它的联络。你们如此强烈地聚焦于物质现实以至它成了你们所知道的唯一现实。

 

When you kill a man, you believe that you kill him forever. Murder is a crime that must then be dealt with. Death, however, does not exist in those terms. In the dawn of physical existence, men knew that death was merely a change of form.

 

当你们杀死一个人，你们相信你们已永远地杀死他了。杀人是一个必须解决的罪行。不过，在其他系统中，死亡并不存在。在肉身存在的初期，人类知道死亡仅仅是一种形式的变化。

 

There is never any justification for violence. There is no justification for hatred. There is no justification for murder. Those who indulge in violence for whatever reason are themselves changed, and the purity of their purpose adultered.

 

暴力决不会是正当的。仇恨决不会是正当的。杀人决不会是正当的。不管出于何种原因，沉溺于暴力的人是他们自己改变了，并且，他们的目的的纯粹性受到了污染。

 

If you do not like the state of your world, it is you yourselves that must change, individually and en masse. This is the only way that change will be effected.

 

如果你们不喜欢你们的世界的状态，是你们自己必须改变，个人地和全体地。这是发生变化的唯一方法。

 

The responsibility for your life and your world is indeed yours. It has not been forced upon you by some outside agency. You form your own dreams and you form your own physical reality. The world is what you are. It is the physical materialization of the inner selves which you have formed.

 

你们的人生和你们的世界的责任事实上由你们负责。这并不是外来力量强加与你们的。你们形成你们自己的梦，你们形成你们自己的物质现实。你们是什么样，世界就是什么样。它是你们形成的内在自我的物质化。

 

It is wrong to curse a flower and wrong to curse a man. It is wrong not to hold any man in honor, and it is wrong to ridicule any man. Your must honor yourselves and see within yourselves the spirit of eternal validity. You must honor all other individuals, because within each is the spark of this validity. When you curse another, you curse yourselves, and the curse returns to you. When you are violent, the violence returns.

 

诅咒一朵花是错误的，诅咒一个人是错误的。轻视任何人是错误的，嘲笑任何人是错误的。你们必须尊重你们自己，看到你们自身中永存的灵魂。你们必须尊敬每一个个人，因为每个人都有这种永存性的火花。当你们诅咒其他人，你们就是诅咒你们自己，诅咒会回到你们身上。当你们使用暴力，暴力会回到你们身上。

 

I speak to you because yours is the opportunity to better world conditions and yours is the time. Do not fall into the old ways that will lead you precisely into the world that you fear.

 

我告诉你们，因为获得更好的世界的机会是你们的，因为时间是你们的。别回到引向你们所害怕的世界的老路上。

 

There is no man who hates but that hatred is reflected outward and made physical, and there is no man who loves but that love is reflected outward and made physical.

 

没有仇恨的人，只有仇恨向外反射并物质化，没有爱的人，只有爱向外反射并物质化。

 

Beyond myself there is another self and still another, of which I am aware. And that self tells you that there is a reality beyond human reality and experience that cannot be made verbal or translated into human terms. And to that self, physical reality is like a warm breath forming in the winter air...

 

在我自己之外，我知道还有另一个自己，另一个之外还有另一个。那个自我告诉你们，在人类现实和经验之外存在一个现实，它不能用人类语言描述和翻译。对于那个自我，物质现实就像在冬天的空气中呼出的一口热气...

 

 

Future Seth Speaks About The Nature And Purpose Of The Communications

未来赛斯谈交流的性质和目的

 

 

The Seth personality is an intermediary. The information already given to you regarding the nature of personality gestalts should make my existence seem a fitting one.

 

赛斯人格是一个媒介。已经传达给你们的有关人格完型性质的信息应使我的存在看上去是适宜的。

 

Seth is what I am, yet I am more than Seth is. Seth is independent, and continues to develop as I do. In the Spacious Present we both exist.

 

我就是赛斯，但我远不止是赛斯。赛斯是独立的，并像我一样继续发展。我们俩都存在于广大呈现中。

 

Seth, as you know him, will always be an element in these communications. He is the connective between us, and he has been a part of me that I have sent out to you. He has participated willingly.

 

赛斯，正如你们所认识的，永远是这些交流中的一个要素。他是我们之间的连接，他是我派到你们那里的我的一部分。他很自愿地参与其中。

 

Names are arbitrary, and we use them merely for your convenience. Seth's name or mine isn't important. Individuality is important and continues in ways you do not suspect.

 

名字是任意的，我们使用它们仅仅是为了方便你们。赛斯的名字或我的名字并不重要。个性是重要的，并以你们不会怀疑的方式延续下去。

 

Seth is learning as I am.

 

赛斯在学习，正如我在学习一样。

 

As an analogy, you could call me a future Seth, at a higher stage of development, however both of us are fully independent and exist simultaneously.

 

作个类比，你们可以把我称作一个未来的赛斯，是处于一个更高的发展阶段，不过，我们两者都完全独立，并且同时存在。

 

These communications, while taking place in your time, are nevertheless responsible in other dimensions for what you would call future developments in your own personalities.

 

但是当这些交流发生在你们的时间里时，它们在其他维度也对你们自己人格中你们所谓的未来发展负责。

 

Whether or not I speak as myself or as Seth, he is the intermediary and the connection between us. He will appear to you as you know him because there are necessary emotional elements that are uniquely his own.

 

不管我是否作为我自己或作为赛斯说话，他是我们之间的媒介和连接。他将以你们所知道的形象向你们显现，因为其中有必需的他自己独有的感情因素。

 

My personality is far different than his but I am also a friend. In many ways I am the same friend. Other portions of me are concerned elsewhere, for I am aware of my existence in other dimensions and keep track of them and direct my many selves.

 

我的人格与他的人格相差甚远，但我也是一个朋友。在许多方面，我是同一个朋友。我的其他部分关心其他地方，因为我知道我在其他维度的存在，并与他们保持联系，并指导我很多的自我。

 

We are Seth and whenever we have spoken we have been known as Seth. We are not alone in this endeavor, for through your centuries other entities like us have also appeared and spoken.

 

我们是赛斯，无论什么时候我们说话，你们知道我们是赛斯。我们的这种努力并不绝无仅有，因为贯穿你们的数个世纪中，象我们一样的其他实体也已出现过、告诉过你们。

 

Our entity is composed of multitudinous selves with their own identities, many of whom have worked in this behalf. Their message will always be basically the same, though the times and circumstances of their communications may differ and be colored accordingly.

 

我们的实体由大量具有各自身份的自我组成，其中很多自我已经为此利益而工作。他们的信息总是基本上相同，虽然他们交流的时代和环境会不同并相应地各具特色。

 

We adopt whatever personality characteristics seem pertinent, for in our own reality we have a bank of complete inner selves, and we are all Seth. We attempt to translate realities into terms you can comprehend. We change our face and form, but we are always the one. Many of us have not been born in the flesh, as I have not been, but in one way we have seeded ourselves through endless universes. The entity had its beginning before the emergence of your time. It was instrumental, with many other entities, in the early formation of energy into physical form. We gave you mental images and upon these images you learned to form the world that you know. We gave you the patterns, intricate and involved from which you form the reality of each physical thing that you know. We taught man to speak before the tongue knew syllables. The entire webwork was initiated by us.

 

我们采用看上去恰当的任何人格个性，因为在我们自己的现实中，我们拥有完整内我的储藏库，而且我们都是赛斯。我们试图把多重现实翻译成你们可以理解的词汇。我们改变我们的面目和形式，但是我们总是那一个。我们中的许多自我并未以肉身出生，我也未以肉身出生过，但我们以某种方式在无尽宇宙中播种我们自己。在你们的时代出现之前实体就已开始。它与很多其他实体一起，帮助能量的早期形式转化成物质形式。我们给了你们意象，你们用这些意象学习形成你们所知道的世界。我们给了你们错综复杂的模式，你们凭借它形成你们所知道的每个物质事物的现实。在人类的舌头还不知道音节时，我们就教人类说话。整个网络系统由我们发动。

 

Our basic knowledge and energy automatically reaches out to nourish all systems that grow.

 

我们的基本的知识和能量自动延伸以滋养成长出来的所有系统。

 

You are like children with a game, and you think that the game is played by everyone. Physical life is not the rule. Identity and consciousness existed long before your earth was formed. You suppose that any personality must appear in physical terms. Consciousness is the force behind matter, and it forms many other realities besides the physical one. It is, again, your own viewpoint that is presently so limited that it seems to you that physical reality is the rule and mode of existence.

 

你们就像玩游戏的孩子们，你们认为每个人都在玩游戏。物质生命并不是唯一规则。在你们地球形成之前身份和意识早就存在了。你们认为任何人格都必须以肉身形式显现。意识是物质背后的力量，除了物质现实之外，它还形成许多其他现实。同样，这只是你们自己目前的如此有限的观点：在你们看来，物质现实是存在的规则和方式。

 

The source and power of your present consciousness has never been physical, and where I am, many are not even aware that such a physical system exists.

 

你们现在的意识的源泉和力量从来不是物质的，在我所在的地方，很多人甚至不知道有这样一个物质系统存在。

 

The physical system is an illusion, but you must accept it and from your viewpoint try to understand the realities that exist beyond it.

 

物质系统是一个幻象，但你们必须接受它，并用你们的观点试图理解在它之外存在的多重现实。

 

You cannot objectify the inner portions of your own identity, and therefore you do not perceive them. So much of your energy is used in the physical productions that you cannot afford to perceive any reality but your own.

 

你们不能将你们自己的身份客体化，因此你们不能感知它们。你们将如此多的能量用于物质生产，以至你们除了你们自己的现实之外无法感知任何现实。

 

Like children playing with blocks, you focus your attention on the physical blocks. The physical blocks appear very real to you when you dwell within their perspective. Other shapes and forms that you could perceive, you do not. Even in explaining other realities, I must use the words “shapes” and “forms” or you would not understand me.

 

就像孩子们玩积木一样，你们将注意力集中于物质的积木。当你们身处它们的范围内时，物质的积木对你们而言非常真实。而其他你们可以看到的形状和形式，你们并没有去看。甚至在解释其他现实时，我必须用“形状”和“形式”这样的词，否则你们就会不理解我。

 

Your idea of progress is building larger blocks, and yet one day you will put aside you “children's toys.”

 

你们进步的观念是搭出更大的积木，然而有朝一日你们将把你们的“儿童玩具”弃置一边。

 

The human race is a stage through which various forms of consciousness travel... Yours is a training system for emerging consciousness. Before you can be allowed into systems of reality that are more extensive and open, you must first learn to handle energy and see through physical materialization, the concrete result of thought and emotion.

 

人类是各种形式的意识在其中旅行的一个的舞台……你们的世界是出现的意识的一个训练系统。在你们获准进入更广大、更开放的现实系统之前，你们必须首先学习处理能量，并识破物质化是思想和感情的具体结果。

 

When you leave the physical system after reincarnations, you have learned the lesson and you are literally no longer a member of the human race, for you elect to leave it. Only the conscious self dwells within it in any case, and other portions of your identity dwell simultaneously within other training systems. In more advanced systems, thoughts and emotions are automatically and immediately translated into action, into whatever approximation of matter there exists. Therefore, the lessons must be taught and learned well.

 

当你们再生结束后离开物质系统时，你们已经学习了功课，你们实际上将不再是人类的一员，因为你们决定离开它。只有意识自我以任何情形居住其内，而你们的身份的其他部分同时居住于其他训练系统之中。在更先进的系统中，思想和感情是自动的，并立即被转换成行动，并成为那里存在的任何事物的近似物。因此，必须好好受教育，好好学习课程。

 

The responsibility for creation must be clearly understood. To some extent you are in a soundproof and isolated room. Hate creates destruction in that “room” and until the lessons are learned, destruction follows destruction... In the terms of other systems, that kind of destruction does not exist - but you believe that it does, and the agonies of dying are sorely felt. It is not that you must be taught not to destroy, for destruction does not actually exist. It is that you must be trained to create responsibly.

 

必须清楚地理解创造的责任。在某种程度上，你们是在一个隔音和隔绝的房间里。在这个“房间”里，仇恨制造破坏，除非学会了功课，否则破坏会跟着破坏……在其他系统中，这种破坏并不存在——但是你们相信它存在，并且会感到强烈的死亡的痛苦。并非是你们必须学会不去破坏，因为破坏实际上并不存在。而是你们必须学会负责任地创造。

 

The weapons of destruction are the obvious things that you see. The counterparts are not so evident, and yet it is the counterparts that are important: the self-discipline learned, the control, the compassion that is finally aroused, and that final and last lesson - the positive desire for creativity and love over destruction and hatred. When this is learned, the cycle is finished.

 

破坏的武器是你们显而易见的。它所对应的却没有如此明显，而它所对应的才是重要的：学会自律、控制、最终唤醒同情心、以及最后一课——破坏和仇恨之上的创造力和爱的积极向上的愿望。当学会了这些，学期就结束了。

 

The training will serve you for existence in a variety of interrelated systems. If the sorrows and agonies within your system were not felt as real, the lesson would not be learned.

 

训练将为你们提供在各种相互关联的系统中的存在。如果在你们的系统中，不能真切地感到悲伤和痛苦，功课就学不会。

 

The teachers within your system are those in their last reincarnation, and other personalities who have left the system but have been assigned to help those still within it.

 

你们系统内的老师是处于他们最后轮回中的人，以及其他已经离开这一系统的人格，但他们受命回来帮助依然在系统之内的人。

 

The system also includes some fragment personalities what are entering for the first time, as well as those in later reincarnations.

 

系统也包括一些片断人格，他们是第一次进入这一系统，也有的是处于更晚的轮回中。

 

Humanity dreams the same dream at once, and you have your mass world. The whole construction is like an educational play in which you are the producers as well as the actors. There is a play within a play within a play. There is no end to the “within” of things. The dreamer dreams, and the dreamer within the dream dreams. But the dreams are not meaningless, and the actions within them are significant. The whole self is the observer and the participator in the roles.

 

人类同时做着同样的梦，你们拥有你们广大的世界。全部构筑就像一幕教育剧，你们是制作者，也是演员。戏中有戏，戏中更有戏中戏。事物“之中”没有尽头。做梦者做梦，梦中的做梦者也在做梦。但梦并非没有意义，梦中的行动意义重大。全我是旁观者和角色的参与者。

 

 

In General, What Is The Purpose Of This Existence?

总的来说，这样存在的目的是什么？

 

 

In the most basic sense, the purpose of life is being - as opposed to not being.

 

从最基本的意义而言，生命的目的是存在——与不存在相对应。

 

In your system of three-dimensional reality you are learning about mental energy (also called thought energy or psychic energy) and how to use it.

 

在你们的三维现实的系统中，你们正在学习心理能量（也叫作思想能量或精神能量）以及如何运用它。

 

Knowledge about mental energy and its use is learned by constantly transforming your thoughts and emotions into physical form (your physical reality) and by then perceiving and dealing with the matter and events that are formed.

 

有关心理能量及其运用的知识是通过不断将你们的思想感情转换成物质形式（你们的物质现实）来进行学习，并通过感知和处理所形成的事物和事件来进行学习。

 

From doing this, you are supposed to get a clear picture of your inner development as it is reflected by the exterior environment.

 

通过学习，你们应该对你们的内在发展有一个清晰的理解，因为它为外在环境所反映。

 

You participate in physical reality so that you can operate and experience within this dimension. Here, you can develop your abilities, learn, create, solve problems and help others.

 

你们参与物质现实，这样你们能在这个维度中运作和经验。在这里，你们能开发你们的能力、学习、创造、解决问题并帮助他人。

 

Matter is the shape that basic experience takes when it comes into your three-dimensional system.

 

当基本经验进入你们的三维系统时，它的形态就是物质。

 

Your dreams, thoughts, expectations, beliefs and emotions are literally transformed into physical matter.

 

你们的梦、思想、期待、信念和情感被精确地转换成物质形态。

 

 

How Does This Work?

这是如何运作的？

 

 

Every nerve and fiber within the body has an unseen inner purpose.

 

身体内的每一根神经和纤维都有一种看不见的内在目的。

 

Nerve impulses travel outward from the body, along invisible pathways, in much the same manner that they travel within the body.

 

神经刺激从身体内部沿着看不见的路径向外传递，它们也以同样方式在体内传递。

 

These pathways are carriers of telepathic thoughts, impulses, and desires containing all the codified data necessary for translating any thought or image into physical actuality, altering seemingly objective events.

Dreams have their own actuality, they not only exist independent of the dreamer, but they also have tangible form, though not in the form of matter as you are familiar with it. They are forever contained as electrically coded data within the cells of the physical organism.

 

正如人格由经验而改变，人格也由梦而改变。我们可以忘记我们的梦，但它们永远是我们的一部分。梦中经验直接由内在自我感觉。梦有它们自己的实在性，它们不仅独立于做梦者而存在，而且它们也有有形的形式，尽管不是你们所熟悉的物质形式。它们作为电码数据永远保存在生物体的细胞之内。

 

The dream universe possesses concepts which will someday completely transform the physical world, but the denial of such concepts as possibilities delays their emergence.

 

梦的宇宙拥有总有一天将完全转化为物质世界的一些概念，但是对这样的概念的否认可能延误它们的物化。

 

There are also shared or “mass” dreams. In these mankind deals with problems of his political and social structure. In these man dreams individually and collectively of ways in which changeabouts could occur. These dreams actually help bring about the resulting change. The very energy and direction of these dreams will help change the situation. The solutions reached are not always the same as those he accepts in the physical world.

 

也有共享的或“大众”的梦。在这些梦中，人类处理他们的政治和社会结构的问题。在这些梦中，人们个人地、集体地做梦，变化在其中得以出现。这些梦实际上帮助带来作为结果的变化。正是这些梦的能量和指导帮助情形的改变。达成的解决方式并不总是和他在物质世界所接受的相同。

 

The energy behind a “bad dream” is the energy of hidden fears, which could be formed by anyone, since there are fears in anyone.

 

“坏的梦”背后的能量是隐藏起来的恐惧能量，任何人可以形成恐惧能量，因为每个人都有恐惧。

 

Evil does not exist separate from the dreamer, and even illness or fear should not be seen as enemies, but rather as aids to understanding and means to a greater end.

 

邪恶并不独立于做梦者而存在，甚至疾病或恐惧不应视作敌人，而更应视作有助于理解和通往更伟大终点的方式。

 

In larger terms there is no evil, only your own lack of perception.

 

大而言之，并不存在邪恶，只是你们自己缺乏理解。

 

Fear directs your conscious energy into the realm of reality of that fear, and you then must deal at that level. The words “May peace be with you.” will get you through any difficulty in other layers of reality.

 

恐惧将你们的意识能量导向那种恐惧的现实王国，然后你们必须在那个层面解决问题。“愿和平与你们同在。”这句话将带你们通过现实的其他层面的任何困难。

 

 

Identity, Personality, Ego And Their Multidimensional Aspects

身份、人格、自我以及它们多维的方面

 

 

Consciousness is not a “thing” in itself, rather, it is a dimension of action made possible by a series of creative dilemmas.

 

意识它自身并不是一个“东西”，而是一个行动的维度，使一系列创造的困境成为可能。

 

There are three dilemmas which represent the areas of reality within which the inner vitality of the universe can express and experience itself.

 

存在三种困境，它们代表现实的区域，宇宙的内在生命力在其中能够表达和经验它自己。

 

The first dilemma is inner vitality's desire and impetus to completely materialize itself, and its inability to do so. This results in action, which is a part of all structure.

 

第一个困境是内在生命力将自己完全物化的欲望和推动力与它无力这么做的困境。这导致行动，行动是所有结构的一部分。

 

Identity may be termed as action which is conscious of itself.

 

身份可以定义为对它自己有意识的行动。

 

An identity is also a dimension of existence, action within action, an unfolding of action upon itself -- and through this interweaving of action with itself, through this re-action, an identity is formed.

 

一个身份也是存在的一个维度，行动之内有行动，行动由其自身展开——并通过这种行动与它自身的交织，通过这种反应，一个身份就形成了。

 

Identity and action cannot be separated.

 

身份和行动无法分开。

 

Without identity, action would be meaningless, for there would be nothing upon which action could act. Action then must, by its very nature, create identities.

 

没有身份，行动将毫无意义，因为行动将无以施加。行动由其性质决定了必须创造身份。

 

Identity, because of its characteristics, will continually seek stability, while stability is impossible. Action would seem to destroy identity, since action must involve change, and any change seems to threaten identity, however, identities are never constant, for consciousness without action would cease to be conscious.

 

由于其特性，身份将持续寻找稳定性，而稳定性是不可能的。行动似乎破坏身份，因为行动必须涉及改变，任何改变似乎威胁身份，然而，身份从不恒定，因为没有行动的意识将停止知觉。

 

Identity must seek stability while action must seek change; yet identity could not exist without change, for it is the result of action and a part of it. This is the second dilemma.

 

身份必须寻找稳定性，而行动必须寻找变化；可身份没有变化就不能存在，因为它是行动的结果和行动的一部分。这是第二个困境。

 

Identity is not the same as individual personality. Personality represents only those aspects of identity which are able to be actualized within three-dimensional existence.

 

身份与个人人格不同。人格只代表身份在三维存在中能够实在化的那些部分。

 

The ego at any give time in this life is simply the part of the inner self that surfaces in physical reality; a group of characteristics that the inner self uses to solve various problems. Even the ego as we think of it changes constantly.

 

在这一生任何时候，自我只是内我浮出物质现实的那部分；这是内我用以解决各种问题的一组特征。正如我们认为的那样，甚至自我也不断改变。

 

Ego consciousness is a state resulting from the third creative dilemma, which happens when consciousness of self attempts to separate itself from action. This involves a state in which consciousness of self attempts to perceive action as an object... and to perceive action, which is initiated by the ego, as a result rather than as a part of ego's own existence. This is impossible since no consciousness or identity can exist without action.

 

自我意识是第三个创造困境所导致的一种状态，当自我意识试图将它自己和行动分开时，它就产生了。这涉及一种状态，自我意识在其中试图将行动作为一件物体感知……并试图感知由自我发起的行动，它作为自我自身存在的一个结果，而非一个部分。这是不可能的，因为没有意识或身份可以离开行动而存在。

 

Each of you exists in other realities and other dimensions, and the self that you call yourself is but a small portion of your entire identity.

 

你们每个人都存在于其他多个现实和其他多个维度中，你叫做你自己的自己只是你完整身份的一小部分。

 

Within the self that you know is the prime identity, the whole self. This whole self has lived many lives and adopted many personalities. Personality may be somewhat molded by the circumstances that are created for it by the whole self but the prime identity uses the resulting experience.

 

在你所知道的自己之内是根本身份，全部的自己。这全部的自己已经生活过很多世生命，采用过很多的人格。人格可以被由全我为它创造的环境所铸就，但根本身份运用所产生的经验。

 

Personality and identity are not dependent upon physical form.

 

人格和身份并不依赖于物质形式。

 

Your prime identity is an energy essence personality which is composed of energy gestalts.

 

你们的根本身份是一个能量本质人格，由能量完型组成。

 

As each individual consciousness grows, out of its experience it forms other “personalities” or fragments of itself. These fragments are entirely independent as to action and decision, while constantly in communication with the whole self of which they are a part.

 

当每个个人的意识成长，它从它的经验中形成其他“人格”或它自己的片断。这些片断完全独立行动和决定，同时与全我不断交流，它们是全我的一部分。

 

These “fragments” themselves grow, develop, and may form their own entities or “personality gestalts”.

 

这些“片断”它们自己成长、发展，并可以形成他们自己的实体或“人格完型”。

 

You have constant contact with the other parts of your whole self, but your ego is so focused upon physical reality and survival within it that you do not hear the inner voices.

 

你一直不断与你的全我的其他部分联络，但你的自我是如此聚焦于物质现实，并生存于其中，以至你听不到内在的声音。

 

No individuality is ever lost. It is always in existence.

 

没有个性会消失。它永远存在。

 

Consciousness is the direction in which the self focuses. Action implies infinite possibilities of focus.

 

意识是自我聚焦的方向。行动意味着聚焦的无限可能性。

 

What you call death is rather your choice to focus in other dimensions and realities.

 

你们所谓的死亡更可以说是你们选择聚焦于其他维度和现实。

 

You do not acquire a “spirit” at death. You are one, now!

 

死亡时你们并不得到一个“灵魂”。你们现在就是一个灵魂！

 

You adopt a body as a space traveler wears a space suit, and for much the same reason.

 

你们穿上身体，正如宇航员穿上宇航服，都是出于同样原因。

 

Each individual from birth forms his own counterpart from built-up, individual, continuous electrical signals that include his dreams, thought, desires and experiences. This counterpart of complete experience is a pattern which is then independent of physical reality. At physical death his personality then exists detached from its physical form.

 

每个个人从出生开始，就用包括他的梦、思维、欲望和经验的增长的、个人的、连续的电信号构成他自己的对应物。这个全部经验的对应物是一个独立于物质现实的模式。生理死亡时，他的人格脱离于它的物质形式而存在。

 

 

Probable Systems Of Reality

可能的现实系统

 

 

For its own purpose, and because of its limitations, the ego chooses a single event from all the probable ones. Until the ego experiences the event it is only one of all the other probable ones. It becomes actual in your reality only when it is experienced by the physical self.

 

为了它自己的目的，也因为它的限制，自我从所有可能事件中选择一个单一事件。这一事件只是全部其他可能事件之一，直到自我经历这一事件。只有当它由物质自我经验时，它在你们的现实中才成为实在。

 

A portion of the whole self can and does experience events in an entirely different fashion than the ego does and is aware of all other probable events that could have been experienced by the ego.

 

全我的一部分能够并确实以一种与自我全然不同的方式经验事件，并且知道所有其它可由自我经验的可能事件。

 

This other portion of the self delves into these alternative events in the same amount of physical time that it takes the ego to experience its chosen event.

 

在自己的这个其他部分让自我经验它所选择的事件的相当的物质时间内，它深入研究这些可选择的事件。

 

These other probable events become just as “real” within other dimensions. There are an infinite number of systems or universes between matter and antimatter. Using the physical senses you can never perceive these other systems, but, probable events are events whether or not you perceive them.

 

这些其他可能事件在其他维度内成为“真实的”。在物质和反物质之间，存在无限的系统或宇宙。你们不能运用生理感官来感知这些其他系统，但可能事件就是事件，无论你们是否感知它们。

 

In dreams, often portions of probable events are experienced in a semiconscious manner. Advanced training in the use of the Inner Senses can also lead to exploration of these systems.

 

在梦中，可能事件的部分常常以半意识的方式经验。对运用内在感觉的高级训练也能导向对这些系统的探索。

 

These systems of probabilities are quite as real as your physical system, you are simply not focused within them.

 

这些可能的系统就像你们的物质系统一样相当的真实，你们只不过没有对准它们聚焦。

 

In these other systems of reality, each of us has “other selves” not an identical self or twin, but other parts of our whole entity, developing abilities in a different way than we are here.

 

在这些其他现实系统中，我们每个人都有“其他的自己”，不是一个相同的自己或双胞胎，而是我们整个实体的其他部分，以与我们这里不同的方式发展能力。

 

Each of these selves experiences time in its own manner according to the nature of its perceptions. Their various realities merge in the overall perceptions of the whole self.

 

这些自己中每一个都以它自己的方式根据它的感知的性质经验时间。它们的各种现实融入全我的全面感知中。

 

There is a constant subconscious interchange of information between all these layers of the whole self.

 

在全我的所有这些层次之间存在一种连续不断的潜意识的信息交换。

 

Ultimately the inner ego tries to bring about comprehension by each of these selves about the other simultaneous selves. Until the whole self is able to perceive its own parts simultaneously, these seemingly separate selves appear, to themselves, isolated and alone.

 

最终内我努力带来每一个这些自己对其他同时存在的自己的理解。直到全我能同时感知它自己的各个部分，这些看似分离的自己，在它们自己看来是隔绝的和孤单的。

 

 

What Is The Nature Of Time?

时间的本质是什么？

 

 

Time as you experience it is an illusion caused by your own physical senses. They force you to perceive action in certain terms, but this is not the nature of action.

 

正如你们所经验的那样，时间是由你们自己的生理感觉造成的一个幻象。它们迫使你们以某种方式感知行动，但这并不是行动的本质。

 

The apparent boundaries between past, present and future are only illusions caused by the amount of action you can physically perceive, and so it seems to you that one moment exists and is gone forever, and the next moment comes and like the one before also disappears. Everything in the universe exists at one time simultaneously.

 

过去、现在和未来之间的明显界线只是由你们能生理感知的行动的数量引起的幻觉，因此在你们看来，一瞬存在然后永远消失了，下一瞬来了，并像它之前的一瞬一样消失。宇宙中的一切在一个时间内同时存在。

 

The first words ever spoken still ring through the universe, and in your terms, the last words ever spoken have already been said. The past, present and future only appear to those who exist within three-dimensional reality.

 

说过的第一句话依然在宇宙中回响，在你们的现实中，要说的最后一句话已经说过。过去、现在和未来只对存在于三维现实内的人显现。

 

The past exists as a series of electromagnetic connections held in the physical brain and in the nonphysical mind.

 

过去作为储存在物质大脑和非物质的思维中的一系列电磁连接而存在。

 

These electromagnetic connections can be changed.

 

这些电磁连接可以改变。

 

The future consists of a series of electromagnetic connections in the mind and brain also. In other words, the past and present are real to the same extent.

 

未来由思维和大脑中的一系列电磁连接构成。换句话说，过去和现在在相同范围内是真实的。

 

You take it for granted that present action can change the future, but present actions can also change the past.

 

现在的行动能改变未来，这在你们看来是理所当然的，但是，现在的行动也能改变过去。

 

The past is no more objective or independent from the perceiver than is the present. The electromagnetic connections were largely made by the individual perceiver. The connection can be changed, and such changes are far from uncommon. These changes happen spontaneously on a subconscious basis.

 

过去对于感知者来说并不比现在更客观、更独立。电磁连接主要由个人感知者造成。连接可以被改变，这样的变化决非罕见。这些变化在一个潜意识的基础上自然发生。

 

The past is seldom what you remember it to be, for you have already rearranged it from the instant of any given event. The past is being constantly re-created by each individual as attitudes and associations change. This is an actual recreation, not a symbolic one. The child is indeed still within the man, but he is not the child that “was”, for even the child within the man constantly changes.

 

过去很少是你们所记住的样子，因为你们已经从任何事件的那一瞬起重新安排它。过去由每个个人因为态度和联想变化而不断重造。这是一种真正的再创造，而不是像一种再创造。孩子实际上依然在大人之内，但是，他不是孩子“曾经是”的样子，因为甚至在大人之内的孩子都不断在变化。

 

Difficulties arise when such alterations do not occur automatically. Severe neurosis is often caused precisely because an individual has not changed his past.

 

当这样的变化并不自动发生时，困难就出现了。严重的神经官能症经常就是一个个人由于没有改变他的过去引起的。

 

A change of attitude, a new association, or any of innumerable other actions will automatically set up new electromagnetic connections and break others.

 

态度的变化、一个新的联想或任何数不清的其他行动将自动建立新的电磁连接，并打破其他连接。

 

Every action changes every other action. Therefore, every action in your present affects actions you call past.

 

每个行动都改变每一个其他行动。因此，你们现在的每个行动都影响你们称作过去的行动。

 

It is possible to react in the past to an event that has not occurred, and to be influenced by your own future.

 

在过去对还未发生的事件作出反应，并将会受到你自己未来的影响，这都是可能的。

 

It is also possible for an individual to react in the past to an event in the future, which in your terms, may never occur.

 

一个个人在过去对一个未来事件作出反应也是可能的，用你们的话来说，这绝不可能发生。

 

Because past, present and future exist simultaneously, there is no reason why you cannot react to an event whether or not it happens to fall within the small field of reality in which you usually observe and participate.

 

因为过去、现在和未来同时存在，并没有你们不能对某一事件作出反应的理由，不论它是否碰巧落入你们通常观察和参与的现实的窄小范围中。

 

On a subconscious level, you react to many events that have not yet occurred as far as your ego's awareness is concerned.

 

在潜意识层次上，你们对许多还未发生的事件作出反应，只要你们的自我感知所能及。

 

Such reactions are carefully screened out and not admitted to consciousness. The ego finds such instances distracting and annoying, and when forced to admit their validity, will resort to the most far fetched rationalizations to explain them.

 

这样的反应被小心地隐藏，意识不准知道。自我发现这种情形令人心烦，当被迫承认它们的有效性时，它寻求合理化来解释它们。

 

No event is predestined. Any given event can be changed not only before and during but after its occurrence.

 

没有事件是注定的。任何事件都能被改变，不仅在发生之前，发生之时，而且在发生之后。

 

The individual is hardly at the mercy of past events, for he changes them constantly. He is hardly at the mercy of future events, for he changes these not only before but after their happening. An individuals future actions are not dependent upon a concrete finished past, for such a past never existed.

 

个人对过去事件几乎毫无怜悯，因为，他不断改变它们。他对未来事件几乎毫无怜悯，因为，他不仅在发生之前，而且在发生之后改变它们。一个个人的未来行动并不依赖于一个具体的已完成的过去，因为这样的过去从未存在过。

 

The past is as real as the future, no more or less.

 

过去和未来一样真实，不多也不少。

 

There is a part of you that is not locked within physical reality, and that part of you knows that there is only an Eternal Now. The part of you that knows is the whole self, your inner and outer ego (all that you are).

 

你们的一部分并未被束缚在物质现实中，你们的这个部分知道只有一个永久的现在。你们这个知道的部分就是全我，你们的内我和外我（你们所是的全部）。

 

From within this framework you will see that see that physical time is as dreamlike as you once thought inner time was. You will discover your whole self, peeping inward and outward at the same “time”, and find that all time is one time, and all divisions, illusions.

 

从这个构架之内，你们将看到物质时间像梦一样朦胧，正如你们曾以为内在时间像梦一样。你们将发现你们的全我，“同时”向内、向外窥视，并发现所有的时间就是一个时间，所有的分割都是幻觉。

赛斯的上帝观念

注：赛斯很保守地使用“上帝”这个词，通常他指的是一切全有或初始能量完形。

 

If you prefer， you can call the supreme psychic gestalt God， but you should not attempt to objectify him.

 

如果你们愿意，你们能把至高的精神完形称作上帝，但是你们不应试图将他客体化。

 

What you call God is the sum of all consciousness， and yet the whole is more than the sum of Its parts.

 

你们所谓的上帝是所有意识的总和，而这个全部超过其各部分之和。

 

He is not one individual， but an energy gestalt. He is a psychic pyramid of interrelated， ever expanding consciousness， that creates simultaneous and instantaneously， universes and individuals that are given duration， psychic comprehension， intelligence and eternal validity.

 

他不是一个个人，而是一个能量完形。他是一个相互关联、不停扩展意识的精神性金字塔，它同时和即时地创造宇宙和被赋予期限、精神性理解力、智能和永恒性个人。

 

Its energy is so unbelievable that is does indeed form all universes; and because its energy is within and behind all universes， fields and systems， it is indeed aware of each sparrow that falls， for it is each sparrow that falls.

 

它的能量是如此难以置信，以至千真万确地形成所有的宇宙；并因为它的能量遍布所有的宇宙、场和系统之内和之后，它确实知道每一只飞落的麻雀，因为它就是每一只飞落的麻雀。

 

Dimly remembered through what you would call history， there was a state of agony in which the powers of creativity and existence were known， but the ways to produce them were not known. All That Is existed in a state of being， but without the means to find expression for Its being. All That Is had to learn this lesson， and could not be taught. From this agony， creativity was originally drawn， and its reflection is still seen. All That Is retains the memory of that state， and it serves as a constant impetus toward renewed creativity. Desire， wish and expectation， therefore， rule all actions and are the basis for all realities. 我不会因为自己要提出一套套的理论而对你们喋喋不休。这些并非只是毫无意义的纯粹的词汇，我想要你们将这些思想赋诸实践。

你们必须努力理解现实的本质。小而言之，我已开始对此进行解释，这些思想，它们自己会令你们思考。要感知其他现实，我们必须运用内在感觉——属于内我的感知方式，无论我们是否拥有物质形式，它都在运作。

 

The Inner Senses expand normal consciousness and allow us to become aware of our own multidimensional existence.

 

内在感觉扩展正常意识并允许我们认识我们自己的多维存在。

 

Undistorted and yours for the asking， is the knowledge inherent in the inner self， pertaining to reality as a whole， its laws， principles and composition.

 

所寻求的未失真的、属于你们自己的知识，是关于作为一个整体的现实、它的规律、法则和组成的内我中固有的知识。

在内我中，你们将发现有关你们所知道的伪装的宇宙的创造的先天知识。你们将发现各种方式方法，存在于心理现实的气候中的内我以此帮助创造存在的各种层次，构造外部感觉以投射和感知这些层次，以及在各种系统中再生的发生方式。在这里，你们也将发现你们自己的答案，比如内我如何为达到它的目的转换能量，如何改变它的形式，如何采纳其他现实，及有关机制。

意识能被转向许多方向，但你们习惯于将它转向某一条道路，你们已忘记还有其他道路。
